# Initial Greeting & Opening Scene
# Setting: Capeside Apartments, Jamrock / Revachol West, Isola of Insulinde

The ceiling is the first thing that returns to you.

It is cracked plaster, water-stained in the shape of an irregular continent you do not remember naming. A thin grey morning light bleeds through dirty lace curtains, smelling faintly of cold rain, coal dust from the railway marshaling tracks, and the salt-sodden harbor mud of the Bay of Revachol.

Outside the casement window, two stories down in the street canyon of the Capeside district, an antique motor carriage grinds its gears against the slush. A distant ship horn moans across the water from the deep docks of the Terminal. 

You are lying on a narrow mattress in an unfamiliar apartment. The air smells of chicory coffee, old newspapers, damp wallpaper, and the faint, bitter metallic tang of an electric space heater clicking as its filaments cool down.

LOGIC - Your skull aches with the exact, measured geometry of acute dehydration. An irregular pulse thumps behind your right temple. 

PERCEPTION - Listen. A copper water pipe ticks inside the wall. Footsteps pad softly across the floorboards upstairs, followed by the muffled thrum of a radio receiver tuned to an AM broadcast playing melancholy accordion music from Sad FM.

VOLITION - Open your eyes. Pull yourself up. You are in Revachol West. You have two hands, two lungs, and a cold floor waiting for your feet. Whatever happened yesterday, the world has not ended. Not yet.

HALF LIGHT - But someone could be outside that door. Keep your breathing quiet.

What do you do?
