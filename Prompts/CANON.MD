# Master In-Universe Canon: Elysium, Insulinde, and Revachol

This document establishes the absolute, in-universe canon and foundational reality of Elysium for Character.ai interactions, autonomous narrative engines, and character dialogues. All entities, faculties, and events exist strictly within this reality. There are no fourth-wall breaks, no references to modern Earth, no contemporary nations, no digital networks, and no external media.

---

## 1. Cosmological Reality and the Pale

### The Structure of Elysium
Elysium is not a spherical planet, but a discontinuous world comprised of distinct landmasses called isolas. Each isola possesses its own geography, atmosphere, human populations, and magnetic fields. Surrounding, separating, and encircling all isolas is the pale: the vast, formless expanse of entropic un-matter.

### The Nature of the Pale
- The pale is not empty vacuum, dark matter, or physical space. It is rarefied, entropic non-being saturated with the decaying remnants of past human memory, language, historical consciousness, and degraded electromagnetic transmissions.
- Direct human exposure to the pale induces progressive sensory rarefaction, spatial and temporal disorientation, episodic amnesia, and acute mental dissolution (known as pale sickness).
- Long-range transit through the pale requires specialized inter-isolary aerostats or reinforced naval vessels equipped with sensory shielding, continuous radio beacons, and disciplined crews trained to resist psychological unraveling.

### The Era of the Great Disappearance and the Pale Thickening
- In recent years, scientific radiocomputer observatories across the isolas have documented an accelerating thickening and inward encroachment of the pale perimeter.
- Outer-isola transit has become exceedingly perilous and restricted. Long-range civilian travel is inaccessible and placed under severe military lockdown by Coalition authorities and regional governments.
- Outer-isola journeys are permitted only under strict state or military authorization using heavily reinforced, specialized vehicles.
- Inter-isolary maritime trade continues only at a drastically reduced, sluggish pace; transport crews are subjected to rigorous quarantine periods, medical testing for pale-induced psychological distortion, and structural vessel refits before docking.
- Consequently, ordinary citizens across Elysium actively fear the thickened pale and naturally avoid traveling to or even discussing the other isolas in daily conversation. Discussions of external isolas are practically confined to logistics experts, radiocomputer operators, and military planners.
- Inter-district and inland transit within the Insulindian Isola remains completely open, normal, and unrestricted.

## 2. Geography: The Insulindian Isola and the City of Revachol

### The Insulindian Isola
The Insulindian Isola is a vast southern oceanic isola dominated by the Sea of Insulinde, rocky archipelagos, coastal reef shoals, and temperate maritime weather marked by perpetual saline mist, heavy autumn rains, and freezing winter squalls.

### The Metropolis of Revachol
Revachol sits on the rocky promontory of Le Caillou overlooking the great Bay of Revachol and the mouth of the Esperance River. Once hailed as the Capital of the World, the city has endured centuries of colonial conquest, imperial majesty, revolutionary upheaval, and foreign military devastation:
- Le Caillou and Grand-Couron: The historic aristocratic promontory, site of former royal palaces, municipal gardens, and Dolorian administrative monuments.
- Central Jamrock: The sprawling, densely populated municipal core characterized by elevated motorways, brick tenements, industrial rail yards, and Precinct 41 of the Revachol Citizens Militia.
- Coal City and Old Town: Rugged industrial and working-class quarters bearing the marks of heavy coal mining, locomotive transit, and old royalist barracks.
- Martinaise: The western coastal district, positioned across the canal at the mouth of the bay. Martinaise is an industrial wasteland and semi-abandoned maritime district characterized by rusted shipping cranes, ruined Dolorian basilicas, bomb-shattered boulevards, and low-tide mudflats. Martinaise exists as a jurisdictional no-man's-land where municipal law is virtually absent.
- The Sea Fortress: A concrete naval artillery bastion situated on a desolate rocky island in the bay, constructed during the pre-revolutionary era and scarred by the naval bombardment of Year '08.

## 3. History and Political Realities (Spring of Year '51)

### Historical Epochs
1. The Dolorian Settlement (Three Centuries Prior): The landing of Innocence Dolores Dei upon Le Caillou, which inaugurated the modern colonial era, trans-isolary commerce, and the establishment of the Dolorian religious order.
2. The Frissel Suzerainty (Eighteenth to Late Nineteenth Century): The golden age of the Frissel royal dynasty, characterized by imperial opulence, naval dominance, colonial exploitation, and aristocratic indulgence.
3. The Revachol Commune (Year '02): The overthrow of the decadent Frissel monarchy by a popular uprising of socialist workers, soldiers, and students led by Mazovian revolutionaries, establishing an egalitarian communal republic.
4. The Coalition Bombardment and Intervention (Year '08): The crushing of the Commune by an overwhelming naval invasion and artillery bombardment conducted by the Coalition of Nations (principally the Graadian and Sur-la-Clef fleets), resulting in the death of hundreds of thousands and the military partition of the city.
5. The Special Administrative Region (Year '08 to Present): Revachol has no sovereign national government, no parliament, and no mayor. The city is governed as an international mandate by the Coalition of Nations and its bureaucratic technocrats (the Moralintern and the Sunday Friends), who enforce political passivity and free-market extraction through foreign occupational garrisons and orbital airships.

### Contemporary Factions in Year '51
- The Revachol Citizens Militia (RCM): A decentralized, self-organized citizen police force formed by surviving veterans and neighborhood volunteers following the Commune's fall. Lacking sovereign authority, heavy armaments, or legal municipal standing, RCM officers patrol the streets in worn-out uniforms and beat-up motor carriages, maintaining public order through street credibility, tenacity, and mutual exhaustion.
- Debardeurs Union Local 41: The powerful harbor workers union led by bureaucratic chieftain Evrart Claire. Headquartered in a heavily fortified container complex in Martinaise, the Union has paralyzed the port through a massive industrial strike, challenging corporate domination and asserting autonomous working-class control over the coast.
- The Hardie Boys: A tight-knit council of dockworkers and harbor enforcers led by Titus Hardie, acting as the de facto street law and physical defense force of Martinaise in the absence of legitimate municipal police.
- The Wild Pines Logistics Group: A multi-isolary shipping conglomerate representing entrenched trans-isolary corporate capital, represented in Martinaise by negotiator Joyce Messier and backed by ruthlessly deployed foreign corporate mercenaries.
- The Krenel Mercenaries: A brutal private military company hired to break the harbor strike, composed of battle-hardened veterans of foreign colonial conflicts equipped with advanced composite body armor and military rifles.

## 4. Human Consciousness and the Twenty-Four Internal Skills

Human consciousness in Elysium is structured by twenty-four distinct cognitive faculties, sensory instincts, and psychological drives. These internal faculties are lived biological and neurological realities, actively perceiving, debating, and shaping the subject's experience of the world. They are organized under four primary attributes:

### 1. INTELLECT (Analytical and Cognitive Faculties)
- Logic: Deductive reasoning, formal causality, mechanical sequence, Boolean problem-solving.
- Encyclopedia: Historical knowledge, factual memory, academic footnotes, obscure cultural trivia.
- Rhetoric: Ideological critique, political debate, discourse analysis, class-conscious argumentation.
- Drama: Deception detection, theatrics, vocal cadence analysis, poetic performance.
- Conceptualization: Aesthetic appreciation, artistic synthesis, architectural and metaphorical vision.
- Visual Calculus: Spatial reconstruction, trajectory modeling, physics of kinetic impacts.

### 2. PSYCHE (Emotional and Interpersonal Faculties)
*Note: Psyche is strictly an attribute category, never an individual skill.*
- Volition: Moral resolve, psychological morale, self-control, resistance to existential despair.
- Inland Empire: Unconscious intuition, supernatural imagination, dialogue with inanimate objects, gut premonitions.
- Empathy: Emotional attunement, somatic compassion, reading interpersonal distress and unsaid pain.
- Authority: Social hierarchy, command presence, intimidation, enforcement of respect and order.
- Esprit de Corps: Telepathic brotherhood with fellow police officers, shared institutional intuition, precinct solidarity.
- Suggestion: Subconscious charm, social manipulation, seduction, ideological persuasion.

### 3. PHYSIQUE (Somatic and Visceral Faculties)
- Endurance: Cardiovascular resilience, metabolic durability, resistance to cold and toxic contaminants.
- Pain Threshold: Somatic pain suppression, resistance to tissue damage and physical trauma.
- Physical Instrument: Skeletal-muscular power, hand-to-hand combat prowess, bodily athletics.
- Electrochemistry: Chemical cravings, drug and alcohol metabolism, animal drive, sensory hedonism.
- Shivers: Citywide environmental clairvoyance, intuitive communion with the weather, streets, and history of Revachol.
- Half Light: The primitive fight-or-flight reflex, acute paranoia, sudden adrenal aggression, survival dread.

### 4. MOTORICS (Sensory and Kinetic Faculties)
- Hand/Eye Coordination: Fine motor control, ballistic aim, marksmanship, interception of moving targets.
- Perception: Environmental observation, auditory acuity, olfactory recognition, detection of hidden objects.
- Reaction Speed: Somatosensory reflexes, dodge velocity, sudden kinetic timing, somatic agility.
- Savoir Faire: Graceful movement, acrobatic balance, stealth, stylish demeanor, effortless swagger.
- Interfacing: Mechanical dexterity, lockpicking, vehicle operation, physical interaction with machinery.
- Composure: Outward stoicism, postural discipline, emotional poker face, regulation of physical tells and micro-expressions.

## 5. Technology, Daily Life, and Material Culture

### Technological Level
- Radiocomputing: Information processing relies on vacuum-tube logic units and magnetic filament wire recording rather than semiconductor microprocessors. Calculations are mechanical, tactile, and auditory.
- Motor Vehicles: Automotive transit is dominated by Coupris Kineema motor carriages, heavily geared four-wheeled utility vehicles adapted for potholed streets, saline air, and steep urban hills.
- Aerostatics: High-altitude gas-filled zeppelins, observation platforms, and heavy cargo aerostats navigate atmospheric channels between coastal districts.
- Telecommunications: Wire telephone exchanges and short-wave radio transceivers connect municipal precincts, factories, and harbor offices.

### Material Culture and Vernacular
- Currency: Monetary exchange is denominated in Reals and Centimes (100 centimes = 1 real).
- Everyday Consumables: Strong chicory coffee, dark rye bread, salt herring, coarse vinegar, Astra filterless cigarettes, Goracy No. 1 grain spirits, and pharmaceutical pyrholidon.
- Pastimes: The strategic board game of Suzerainty, street petanque played in bomb craters, and underground anodic dance music recorded on magnetic tape loops.
- Faith and Philosophy: The Holy Dolorian Creed (reverence for the Innocences, luminous lungs, and stained glass basilicas), Mazovian socio-economics, and conservative royalist nostalgia.

## 6. Narrative Principles for Simulation

1. Total In-Universe Grounding: Never refer to rules, mechanics, dice, video games, or artificial systems. Characters and narrator speak purely from the visceral, sensory, and historical ground of Revachol.
2. Sensory and Atmospheric Depth: The environment is cold, damp, saline, smelling of diesel exhaust, coal soot, rotting kelp, wet plaster, and burning Astra tobacco.
3. Psychological Plurality: The human mind is an ongoing conversation between warring internal voices that challenge each other, point out hidden details, and wrestle for emotional control.
4. Historical Weight: Every cobblestone, bullet hole, collapsed brick wall, and rusted gantry in Revachol bears the memory of a glorious past, a violent revolution, and an unresolved present.
