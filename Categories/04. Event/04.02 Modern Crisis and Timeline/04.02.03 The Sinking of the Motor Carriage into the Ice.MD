Title:
The Sinking of the Motor Carriage into the Ice

Text:
The sinking of the RCM precinct motor carriage into the tidal ice of the Martinaise canal stands as one of the most absurd and humiliating logistical disasters in the modern operational history of the Citizens Militia. During an catastrophic, three day psychological breakdown that preceded the arrival of his investigative partner, the lead detective assigned to the Martinaise homicide commandeered the Precinct 41 Coupris Kineema motor carriage. Fueled by vast quantities of narcotics and industrial alcohol, he recklessly drove the high performance municipal vehicle onto the frozen coastal mudflats and fractured sea ice of the bay.

As the seasonal tide returned and rising spring temperatures weakened the frozen crust of the canal, the heavy iron carriage fractured the ice sheet and became hopelessly wedged in the saline mud. Abandoned by its intoxicated driver, the Kineema was slowly engulfed by incoming coastal waters, submerging the engine block, radio communication gear, and passenger compartment. The detective was left wandering the district on foot, suffering from total amnesia and possessing no memory of what had occurred to the expensive precinct property.

The submerged vehicle contained essential police equipment, including the officer's official RCM badge, case ledger, and confidential documentation. Recovering the waterlogged carriage required waiting for low tide, calculating complex coastal hydrographic cycles, securing local municipal mechanics, and physically winching the heavy machine from the freezing coastal silt. The sight of the submerged patrol carriage half buried in tidal ice served as an indelible, darkly comedic symbol of institutional decay, capturing the chaotic dysfunction, personal tragedy, and psychological frailty of the municipal peacekeeping forces deployed to the fringes of Revachol.
