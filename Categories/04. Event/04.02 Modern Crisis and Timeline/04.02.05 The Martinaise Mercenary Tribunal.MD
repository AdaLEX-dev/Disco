Title:
The Martinaise Mercenary Tribunal

Text:
The Martinaise Mercenary Tribunal was the violent, climactic confrontation that erupted in the snow dusted square outside the Whirling-in-Rags, pitting armed corporate mercenaries against the union dockworkers council. Consumed by grief, alcohol, and combat stimulants following the murder of their commander, the surviving Krenel private contractors, led by the brutal veteran Kortenaer and the psychotic gunner Ruud Hoogenstraten, donned heavy Fairweather ceramic body armor and marched into the district center to execute an unsanctioned military tribunal against Titus Hardie and his men.

Confronted with imminent slaughter, Titus Hardie, his loyal enforcers, and local cafeteria workers stood their ground armed with hunting rifles, pistols, and blunt instruments. The RCM investigative detachment, consisting of Lieutenant Kim Kitsuragi and his partner, bravely stepped into the freezing square between the two heavily armed factions in a desperate effort to mediate the dispute and prevent a massacre. When negotiations collapsed under Kortenaer's unhinged fury, the square disintegrated into a ferocious, close quarters firefight.

High velocity automatic rifle bursts and heavy pistol rounds tore across the cobblestone plaza, shattering hostel windows and ripping through masonry and flesh alike. Despite their superior training, military firearms, and heavy ceramic armor, the mercenaries suffered devastating casualties as the union dockworkers fought back with desperate, cornered ferocity. The bloody encounter resulted in multiple deaths on both sides, severe gunshot wounds to the investigating militia officers, and the permanent destruction of civic peace in Martinaise. The tribunal represented the ultimate culmination of post war tension, where corporate violence and working class resistance inevitably bathed the streets of Revachol in blood.
