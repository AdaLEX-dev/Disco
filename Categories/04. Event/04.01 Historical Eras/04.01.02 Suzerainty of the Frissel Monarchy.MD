Title:
Suzerainty of the Frissel Monarchy

Text:
The Suzerainty of the Frissel Monarchy established Revachol as the wealthiest and most decadent sovereign empire in the known world, dominating maritime trade across the Insulindian Isola for over two centuries. Commencing with the coronation of King Frissel I, the royalist dynasty consolidated absolute political authority, aristocratic patronage, and colonial resource extraction from its magnificent court at the Palais de l'Esperance on Le Caillou. The suzerainty commanded a formidable naval armada, using gunboat diplomacy and commercial monopolies to dominate merchant corridors and extract immense tributary wealth from surrounding archipelagos.

Under subsequent monarchs, the crown devolved into extravagant opulence, financial recklessness, and systemic moral indifference toward the impoverished working classes. The reign of King Filippe III, historically remembered as Filippe the Opulent or the Cocaine King, epitomized this aristocratic decline. While royal courtiers indulged in continuous palace balls, botanical extravagances, and chemical hedonism, the industrial districts of Revachol West, Jamrock, and Martinaise suffered from catastrophic overcrowding, deadly cholera outbreaks, and violent labor exploitation.

The crown maintained order through the King's Carabinier regiment, a ruthless royalist gendarmerie that crushed urban unrest with cavalry charges and summary imprisonment. This extreme social polarization, coupled with the ruinous economic drain of foreign military adventures, eroded the traditional loyalty of the urban proletariat. By the close of the century, the Frissel monarchy had transformed into a hollow, despised feudal relic. The gilded corruption of the royal court directly catalyzed the revolutionary explosion of the Revachol Commune, terminating centuries of divine monarchic rule.
