Title:
The Coalition Naval Bombardment of Year 08

Text:
The Coalition Naval Bombardment of Year '08 marked the catastrophic military intervention that violently extinguished the Revachol Commune and initiated decades of foreign colonial occupation. Alarmed by the spread of revolutionary socialism in the Insulindian Isola, a reactionary alliance of foreign capitalist powers, known as the Moralist Coalition, dispatched a massive naval battle fleet consisting of ironclad dreadnoughts and combat aerostats to crush the popular uprising. Commencing in the spring of '08, Coalition warships encircled the Bay of Revachol and subjected the defenseless coastal districts to weeks of continuous offshore artillery shelling.

Heavy high explosive naval ordnance decimated the architectural fabric of Revachol West. Martinaise, Grand-Couron, and Old Town absorbed the brunt of the bombardment, suffering the destruction of coastal docks, municipal bridges, and historic Dolorian stone structures. Millions of bricks collapsed into the streets, burying thousands of civilian families and Red Army defenders beneath mountainous ruins. Dense clouds of black pulverized soot obscured the sun over the estuary, creating a nightmarish landscape of flame, falling rubble, and deafening concussive shockwaves.

Following the offshore barrage, Coalition landing brigades composed of foreign infantry, naval marines, and motorized gun carriages stormed the shattered shoreline to execute relentless mop up operations against the remaining pockets of communard resistance. The bombardment broke the military cohesion of the Commune, leaving the coastal districts shattered, depopulated, and permanently scarred. The physical ruins and shrapnel-pocked facades visible throughout modern Martinaise remain raw, unhealed monuments to the overwhelming industrial violence unleashed by the Coalition armada.
