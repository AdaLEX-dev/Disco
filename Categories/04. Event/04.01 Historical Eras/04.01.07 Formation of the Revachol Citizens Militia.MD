Title:
Formation of the Revachol Citizens Militia

Text:
The formation of the Revachol Citizens Militia emerged from the smoldering vacuum of civic order following the collapse of the Commune and the establishment of Coalition military occupation. With traditional municipal police forces dissolved and foreign occupation garrisons refusing to police ordinary civilian crimes, the streets of Revachol devolved into lawless chaos dominated by armed gangs, organized criminal syndicates, and vigilante retribution. In response, neighborhood defense councils, demobilized veterans, and volunteer safety brigades organized grassroots peacekeeping networks to restore basic safety.

Recognizing their inability to pacify the sprawling metropolis through military force alone, Coalition occupation administrators reluctantly granted de facto recognition to these volunteer brigades, officially chartering them as the Revachol Citizens Militia. Operating under severe legal, logistical, and budgetary constraints, the RCM was explicitly prohibited from maintaining heavy military armaments, automatic weapons, armored fighting vehicles, or air support, ensuring that the volunteer force remained strictly subservient to foreign imperial authority. Precints were established throughout the urban districts, including Precinct 41 in Jamrock and Precinct 57 in Couron.

Despite chronic underfunding, dilapidated precinct houses, and moral compromise, the RCM evolved into a complex, resilient institution. Its sworn officers navigate an impossible jurisdictional tightrope between corrupt local dock unions, predatory corporate mercenaries, hostile street youth, and contemptuous foreign authorities. Possessing no formal constitutional mandate yet serving as the sole functioning buffer between total social collapse and communal survival, the RCM embodies the tragic, enduring resilience of post-war Revachol.
