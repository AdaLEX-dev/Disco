Title:
The Antecentennial War

Text:
The Antecentennial War was a devastating interisolary conflict that raged across multiple landmasses in the decades preceding the turn of the century, profoundly altering the geopolitical balance of Elysium. Driven by commercial rivalry, territorial expansion, and imperial prestige between rival coalitions of monarchist powers and emerging corporate monopolies, the war demanded total industrial and human mobilization. Revachol, as the preeminent naval and economic power of the Insulindian Isola, committed hundreds of thousands of conscripted colonial subjects, merchant vessels, and immense industrial resources to distant combat theaters.

The prolonged conflict inflicted catastrophic economic strain upon the civilian population of Revachol. Naval blockades disrupted interisolary supply lines, leading to severe food shortages, hyperinflation, and the widespread rationing of coal and essential medicines across the capital. Working class neighborhoods in Jamrock, Coal City, and Martinaise were ravaged by malnutrition and industrial accidents as factories worked continuously to produce artillery shells, naval armaments, and military transport equipment for distant battlefields.

Rather than securing imperial glory, the Antecentennial War thoroughly delegitimized the ruling Frissel dynasty. Thousands of wounded and disillusioned conscripts returned to Insulinde only to find mass unemployment, ruined families, and callous aristocratic indifference. The harrowing experience of industrialized warfare shattered traditional deference to monarchic authority, fostering revolutionary solidarity among frontline soldiers and maritime dockworkers. The organizational networks forged during anti war strikes and veterans mutual aid leagues created the ideological and logistical foundation for the revolutionary upheaval that toppled the crown.
