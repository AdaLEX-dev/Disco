Title:
Fairweather T-56 Body Armor

Text:
Fairweather T-56 Body Armor is an advanced, military-grade powered exoskeleton and composite personal protection system manufactured by Fairweather Security, an international defense contractor based in Mundi. Representing the pinnacle of modern protective engineering, the suit was designed to provide private mercenary forces and corporate security units with absolute tactical superiority during urban counter-insurgency operations, industrial pacification campaigns, and hostile crowd suppression.

The armor system comprises an interlocking suite of contoured ceramic breastplates, ballistic composite greaves, articulated gauntlets, and an enclosed reinforced helmet. Each component is fabricated from layered ultra-dense silicon carbide ceramic bonded to a Kevlar weave backing, capable of absorbing direct kinetic impacts from high-caliber rifle rounds, shrapnel detonations, and high-velocity bludgeoning without structural penetration. The suit incorporates an internal pneumatic servo skeleton powered by compact rechargeable micro-accumulator cells, which augments the wearer's physical lifting strength, absorbs kinetic shock from jumping or falling, and dampens physical fatigue during prolonged deployments.

The interior of the suit features climate-controlled thermal padding and integrated biometric sensor nodes that monitor heart rate, body temperature, and adrenaline levels. Visually, the T-56 presents an intimidating, insectoid silhouette of matte olive-drab or charcoal plates adorned with corporate serial numbers. In Revachol, the armor serves as the unmistakable battle dress of the Krenel corporate mercenaries deployed by the Wild Pines Group to break the Debardeurs Union dock strike in Martinaise, functioning as an imposing symbol of foreign corporate dominance and sovereign impunity.
