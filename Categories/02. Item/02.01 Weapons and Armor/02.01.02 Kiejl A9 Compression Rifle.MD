Title:
Kiejl A9 Compression Rifle

Text:
The Kiejl A9 Compression Rifle is a specialized, military-grade sniper and marksmanship weapon engineered for silent long-range target interdiction. Originally developed in Graad during the late Antecentennial War and manufactured in limited numbers by specialized armories, the rifle differs radically from conventional propellant firearms by utilizing a high-pressure pneumatic compression system rather than combustible gunpowder to propel its munitions.

The weapon is constructed around a heavy fluted barrel machined from hardened vanadium steel, encased within an elongated aerodynamic shroud that integrates multi-stage expansion baffles to suppress pneumatic exhaust. The action is powered by a high-pressure gas reservoir cylinder mounted parallel beneath the barrel, charged using a manual hand pump or pneumatic compressor to over three hundred atmospheres. When fired, a precision magnetic solenoid valve releases a microscopic burst of compressed gas that accelerates a dense, fin-stabilized seven-point-six-two millimeter steel or tungsten dart to supersonic velocities with virtually zero audible muzzle signature or muzzle flash.

The tactical lethality of the Kiejl A9 is renowned among military historians and forensic ballistics investigators. Firing from concealed positions at ranges exceeding six hundred meters, the rifle delivers flat, laser-straight trajectories unaffected by coastal crosswinds. The absence of gunpowder residue, chemical propellant smoke, or loud acoustic reports renders the origin of fire nearly impossible to locate through standard forensic triangulations. In the history of Revachol, the weapon achieved dark infamy as the clandestine instrument utilized from the derelict Sea Fortress Island to conduct strategic political assassinations across the water in Martinaise.
