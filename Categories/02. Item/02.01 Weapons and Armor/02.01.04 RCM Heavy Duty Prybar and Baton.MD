Title:
RCM Heavy Duty Prybar and Baton

Text:
The RCM Heavy Duty Prybar and Baton are the foundational mechanical utility and physical enforcement implements carried by patrol officers of the Revachol Citizens Militia. Manufactured under municipal procurement contracts by industrial metalworks in Coal City, these standardized tools reflect the harsh reality of urban policing in a post-revolutionary metropolis where officers frequently operate without motorized backup or specialized breaching equipment.

The prybar, designated officially as the RCM Utility Lever Model 38, is forged from a single solid bar of high-tensile carbon steel measuring eighty-five centimeters in length. It features a beveled chisel wedge at one extremity and an angled gooseneck claw at the opposing end, finished in a weather-resistant black industrial lacquer. Engineered to withstand tremendous mechanical shear force, the tool is utilized daily by patrolmen to force jammed apartment doors, pry open rusty shipping container latches, unseat frozen manhole covers, and breach barricaded entryways across the neglected wards of Jamrock and Martinaise. Its substantial physical weight also allows it to serve as a formidable defensive club when officers are cornered in dark alleys.

Complementing the prybar is the RCM Standard Service Baton, a sixty-centimeter impact weapon composed of a weighted lead core encased within molded vulcanized neoprene rubber. Designed to deliver disabling blunt force to an assailant's muscular motor points without causing lethal skull fractures, the baton features a textured knurled grip and an adjustable leather wrist lanyard. Carried in heavy leather belt scabbards, these two implements symbolize the daily, grinding physical authority wielded by the militia across the cracked cobblestones and unlit tenement hallways of West Revachol.
