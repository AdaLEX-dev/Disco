Title:
RCM Blue Ledger and Clipboard

Text:
The RCM Blue Ledger and Clipboard constitute the essential bureaucratic casework documentation system issued to detectives and senior patrol officers of the Revachol Citizens Militia. Originating during the administrative reorganization of the militia following the Coalition intervention of '08, the ledger serves as the official, court-mandated physical repository for all field observations, witness interrogations, evidence manifests, and case progress notes compiled during criminal inquiries.

The ledger itself is an oblong notebook bound in durable, water-resistant dark blue pebbled leather stamped on the front cover with the brass municipal crest of Revachol and the insignia of the RCM. Inside, the volume contains two hundred numbered pages of heavy, acid-free rag paper printed with fine blue grid lines, designed to accept fountain pen ink and graphite without bleeding or smearing under wet coastal conditions. A thick woven elastic band secures the volume when closed, while an internal gusseted pocket stores folded arrest warrants, carbon-copy receipts, forensic sketches, and crime scene photographic prints.

Accompanying the ledger is a heavy-duty clipboard fabricated from pressed aluminum or rigid vulcanized fiberboard, fitted with a high-tension spring-steel paper clamp. For a militia detective navigating the hostile, divided neighborhoods of Revachol West, the Blue Ledger represents far more than a simple record book; it is a tangible badge of sovereign legal authority, a psychological shield during tense interrogations, and a personal confession journal wherein an officer records their deductions, moral doubts, and casework milestones while attempting to impose order upon urban chaos amidst the relentless coastal rain, peeling wallpaper, and crumbling masonry of West Revachol.
