Title:
Coupris Kineema Motor Carriage

Text:
The Coupris Kineema Motor Carriage is an iconic, heavy-duty utility motor vehicle manufactured by the Coupris Automotive Corporation in Grand-Couron, widely recognized as the primary motorized patrol transport of the Revachol Citizens Militia. Designed in the late nineteen-thirties to navigate the rutted, uneven streets and post-war rubble of Greater Revachol, the Kineema combines industrial durability with agile urban mobility, serving as a versatile mobile station for patrol officers across the capital.

The vehicle is built upon a reinforced steel ladder chassis supporting an angular, welded sheet-metal cab finished in the standard dark blue and cream livery of the municipal militia. It features a unique three-wheeled configuration, with a single heavy front steering wheel controlled by hydraulic floor levers rather than a traditional circular steering wheel, providing an extraordinarily tight turning radius through narrow tenement alleyways. Power is generated by a front-mounted, air-cooled four-cylinder boxer combustion engine that runs on standard commercial kerosene or low-octane motor spirits, producing a characteristic rhythmic clatter that echoes off brick facades blocks before the vehicle comes into view.

The interior cab accommodates two officers on heavy leather bucket seats, positioned before a dashboard equipped with a vacuum-tube police radio transceiver, emergency siren toggle switches, and a mechanical mileage chronometer. The rear cargo deck houses a locked galvanized steel equipment chest containing spare Villiers munitions, road flares, tow cables, and heavy FALN flashlights. Sturdy and mechanically resilient, the Kineema is capable of plowing through winter slush, mounting granite curbs, and enduring street violence with uncomplaining reliability.
