Title:
FALN Heavy LED Flashlight

Text:
The FALN Heavy LED Flashlight is a high-performance portable illumination instrument engineered by FALN, the premier athletic and industrial technical equipment corporation based in Oranjestad. Originally developed for deep-sea salvage divers, commercial cavern explorers, and emergency municipal rescue teams, the flashlight has become the gold standard illumination tool utilized by investigative officers of the Revachol Citizens Militia and commercial harbor technicians across West Revachol.

The body of the flashlight is precision-machined from a solid billet of aerospace-grade anodized aluminum alloy, featuring deep non-slip cross-hatch knurling and fluted cooling fins around the bezel. The forward optical assembly contains a cluster of high-intensity solid-state light-emitting diodes protected behind a tempered mineral glass lens, capable of projecting a piercing white beam exceeding five hundred lumens through dense coastal fog, driving sleet, and dust-choked industrial interiors. The device is fully sealed against moisture, saltwater corrosion, and mechanical shock by thick silicone O-rings, allowing it to withstand total submersion in canal muck or concrete impacts from two meters height.

Power is supplied by an internal rechargeable nickel-cadmium battery pack housed inside the screw-cap barrel, providing up to six hours of continuous high-output illumination. In the perpetual dimness of Martinaise, where municipal streetlights have remained unpowered for over fifty years, the FALN flashlight functions as an indispensable investigative weapon. Detectives rely on its focused, high-contrast beam to inspect blood spatter patterns, examine lock tumbler scratches, and navigate the pitch-black subterranean labyrinths beneath the Doomed Commercial Area and the Feld Building.
