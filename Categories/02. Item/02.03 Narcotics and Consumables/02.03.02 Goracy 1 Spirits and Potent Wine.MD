Title:
Goracy 1 Spirits and Potent Wine

Text:
Goracy 1 Spirits and Potent Wine represent the primary high-proof distilled and fermented alcoholic beverages consumed across the working-class wards and coastal waterfronts of Revachol. Manufactured to satisfy the intense demand for cheap, rapid intoxication among manual laborers, sailors, and impoverished retirees, these industrial spirits are available in every tavern, grocery stall, and unlicensed back-alley kiosk throughout Jamrock and Martinaise.

Goracy 1, known colloquially as the Graadian Heater or simply the One, is an industrial-strength grain and root spirit distilled according to traditional Northern Graadian recipes. Bottled in heavy amber glass bottles sealed with stamped tin caps, the clear liquid possesses an alcohol concentration exceeding fifty-five percent by volume. It delivers a harsh, burning sensation in the throat, smelling intensely of raw grain alcohol, fusel oils, and medicinal camphor. A single mouthful induces a swift, numbing warmth across the chest, making it an essential chemical shield against hypothermia for debardeurs working exposed harbor cranes during sub-zero winter gales.

Complementing Goracy 1 is cheap Revacholian Potent Wine, marketed under various local labels including Saint-Sans Red and Commodore Coastal Reserve. Produced from bulk grape must and fortified with industrial spirits to reach eighteen percent alcohol, this thick, dark purple wine stains the teeth and tongue while tasting heavily of fermented sugar, sulfur preservatives, and oak tannins. In the bleak economic climate of the post-bombardment era, these potent libations provide thousands of traumatized citizens with temporary chemical oblivion, fueling barroom brawls, tragic street addiction, and quiet despair along the foggy avenues of the bay on long winter nights.
