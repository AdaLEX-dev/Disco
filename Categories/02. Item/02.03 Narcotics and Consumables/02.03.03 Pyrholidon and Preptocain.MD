Title:
Pyrholidon and Preptocain

Text:
Pyrholidon and Preptocain are potent synthetic pharmaceutical compounds and high-potency street narcotics widely circulated across the Insulindian Isola. Originating as specialized chemical formulations developed by military laboratories during the Antecentennial War, both substances have migrated into the black market and urban drug trade of Greater Revachol, where they are consumed by individuals seeking cognitive enhancement, sensory escape, or chemical stamina.

Pyrholidon is a complex poly-synthetic psychoactive drug distributed in tiny, sealed blue glass ampoules or blister-pack gelatin capsules. Originally synthesized as a radioprotective and pale-retardant compound to shield interisolary airship pilots from the disorienting, memory-eroding effects of deep pale transit, the chemical profoundly alters human neurological perception. Upon ingestion or subcutaneous injection, Pyrholidon heightens the user's sensitivity to abstract symbolism, psychic hunches, and eerie sensory resonance, while producing vivid waking dreams and a profound detachment from physical reality. However, chronic consumption leads to severe neuro-chemical dependency, synaptic degradation, and creeping paranoia.

Preptocain, by contrast, is a powerful synthetic central nervous system stimulant belonging to the speed class of pharmaceuticals. Distributed as a bitter white crystalline powder or pressed white pills, it floods the human body with artificial dopamine and adrenaline, eliminating physical fatigue, sharpening kinetic reaction speed, and inducing aggressive euphoria. Heavily abused by night-shift longshoremen, lorry drivers navigating the motorways, and delinquent youth gangs in Martinaise, Preptocain is notorious for causing violent tremors, cardiac stress, and severe psychological crashes once the chemical burn recedes.
