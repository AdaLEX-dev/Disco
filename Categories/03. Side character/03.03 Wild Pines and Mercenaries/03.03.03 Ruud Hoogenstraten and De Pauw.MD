Title:
Ruud Hoogenstraten and De Pauw

Text:
Ruud Hoogenstraten and De Pauw constitute the lethal operational vanguard of the Krenel corporate mercenary detachment deployed under Colonel Raul Kortenaer in Martinaise. As elite combat contractors operating under corporate charter from the Wild Pines Group, these two professional soldiers represent the chilling reality of modern privatized warfare, combining state-of-the-art military technology with ideological fanaticism and psychological depravity.

Ruud Hoogenstraten, known as the Gun-Collector, is a notorious, psychopathic sniper and ideological extremist originating from the reactionary fringes of foreign colonial armies. Completely entombed within a matte-black Fairweather T-56 ceramic armor suit with an opaque blast visor, Ruud communicates in guttural, violent outbursts, driven by virulent racial chauvinism and a pathological compulsion to kill. He collects ballistic trophies from his victims and views the impoverished residents of Martinaise as subhuman targets awaiting extermination, demonstrating absolute eagerness to open fire on unarmed crowds.

De Pauw, by contrast, operates as the squad's cold, tactical radio operator and heavy weapons specialist. Clad in identical powered ceramic armor, De Pauw maintains rigorous military discipline, monitoring Coalition radio frequencies and coordinating fire sectors with mechanical detachment. He carries an arsenal of high-explosive ordnance and military flechette rounds. While less overtly psychotic than Ruud, De Pauw exhibits an utter absence of human empathy, executing lethal orders with corporate efficiency. Together with Kortenaer, this armored duo forms a terrifying kinetic shock unit whose violent collision with the Hardie Boys turns the Martinaise municipal square into a slaughterhouse beneath the coastal sky.
