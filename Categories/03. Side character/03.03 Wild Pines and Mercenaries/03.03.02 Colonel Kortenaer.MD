Title:
Colonel Kortenaer

Text:
Colonel Raul Kortenaer, known colloquially among his troops as the Red Commander, is the field commander of the private mercenary detachment deployed to Martinaise by the Wild Pines Group to suppress the Debardeurs Union strike. A battle-hardened veteran of brutal colonial brush wars and counter-insurgency operations in the southern isola of Semenine, Kortenaer is an unstable, alcohol-ravaged professional soldier consumed by vengeful fury following the murder of his brother-in-arms, Ellis Lely.

Encased within an imposing suit of Fairweather T-56 ceramic powered body armor, Kortenaer represents the apex of modern private military lethality. His physical body is heavily scarred, his face lined by decades of hard drinking, sleep deprivation, and extreme combat trauma. Following the lynching of Lely behind the Whirling-in-Rags, Kortenaer descends into a state of chronic, heavily armed intoxication, quartering his squad in an armored motor transport while guzzling industrial spirits and plotting a devastating reprisal against the local populace. He violently curses the Coalition authorities and the local militia alike for failing to deliver his brother's killer. His speech is laced with military slang, violent threats, and bitter memories of past colonial massacres.

Kortenaer's military doctrine is devoid of ethical constraint, viewing civilian populations as hostile insurgent combatants subject to total kinetic pacification. Bound to his deceased comrade by fierce martial fraternity and shared wartime atrocities, he refuses corporate orders from Wild Pines to stand down. Armed with high-caliber automatic weapons and supported by heavily armored squad mates, Kortenaer marches toward an inevitable, bloody confrontation in the central square of Martinaise, determined to drown the Hardie Boys and anyone standing with them in overwhelming gunfire.
