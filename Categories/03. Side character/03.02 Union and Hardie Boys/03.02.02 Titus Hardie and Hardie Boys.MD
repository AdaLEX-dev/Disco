Title:
Titus Hardie and Hardie Boys

Text:
Titus Hardie and the Hardie Boys constitute an informal, armed labor defense council and community vigilante group operating under the auspices of the Debardeurs Union in Martinaise. In the total absence of a functioning municipal police precinct in the district, this tight-knit brotherhood of dockers, crane mechanics, and warehousemen serves as the de facto law enforcement and peacekeeping authority across the neighborhood, operating from a permanent booth inside the Whirling-in-Rags cafeteria.

Titus Hardie, the undisputed leader of the group, is a broad-shouldered, gravel-voiced dockworker defined by his heavy denim jacket, thick mustache, and unyielding working-class pride. Fiercely protective of Martinaise civilians, Titus commands absolute loyalty from his crew, which includes hardened longshoremen such as Eugene, Alain, Theo, Glen, and the portly radio enthusiast Angus. The Hardie Boys enforce neighborhood order through physical intimidation, resolving domestic disputes, expelling violent drug pushers, and maintaining communal safety while remaining deeply suspicious of outside authorities, foreign corporate mercenaries, and RCM militia detectives.

Armed with heavy wrenches, industrial prybars, and concealed surplus pistols, the group embodies the stubborn communal solidarity of Revachol's proletariat. A portable cassette player frequently hums on their table, filling the cafeteria with gritty rock tunes. When a brutal lynching behind the Whirling threatens to ignite a catastrophic war with corporate mercenaries, the Hardie Boys close ranks, fabricating a coordinated cover story to protect a neighborhood woman from brutal mercenary retribution. Despite their rough demeanor and street-level justice, Titus and his men represent the last line of defense standing between the impoverished residents of Martinaise and violent annihilation.
