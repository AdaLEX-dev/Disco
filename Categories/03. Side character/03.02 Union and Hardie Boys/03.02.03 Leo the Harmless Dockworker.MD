Title:
Leo the Harmless Dockworker

Text:
Leo is a gentle, simple-minded, and universally beloved municipal dockworker employed by the Debardeurs Union Local 41 in the commercial harbor of Martinaise. Serving as an informal clerk, errand runner, and logistical assistant to Union Boss Evrart Claire, Leo occupies a humble wooden dispatch shack near the harbor container gates, where he distributes tools, sorts keys, and welcomes visitors with relentless, unfeigned warmth.

Dressed in a faded yellow worker's boiler suit, oversized rubber boots, and a stained woolen cap, Leo presents a striking contrast to the hardened, suspicious longshoremen who guard the maritime perimeter. He possesses a remarkably innocent, trusting disposition and a childlike enthusiasm for routine clerical tasks. His mind operates with literal-minded simplicity, devoid of political cynicism, malice, or ideological prejudice. While the surrounding district is gripped by bitter labor strikes, mercenary threats, and deep-seated paranoia, Leo remains completely unperturbed, cheerfully sweeping gravel, polishing brass padlocks, and offering kind words to anyone who passes his post. He takes immense pride in organizing keyrings for the Capeside apartment blocks and cataloging spare grease fittings for the gantry cranes.

Despite his intellectual limitations, Leo is deeply cherished and protected by the entire Martinaise community. The dockworkers, union enforcers, and local shopkeepers treat him with fraternal affection, shielding him from the violent realities of the ongoing standoff between the union and Wild Pines. His total inability to tell a convincing lie or comprehend malicious deceit makes him a refreshingly pure, if occasionally frustrating, source of information for investigators navigating the treacherous political intrigues of the harbor.
