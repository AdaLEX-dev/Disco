Title:
Evrart Claire

Text:
Evrart Claire is the formidable, immensely influential head of the Debardeurs Union Local 41, exercising near-total political and economic control over the Martinaise commercial harbor and the surrounding coastal wards of West Revachol. Working in seamless tandem with his twin brother Edgar Claire, Evrart has transformed a fragmented labor union into a powerful geopolitical state-within-a-state capable of challenging multinational corporate conglomerates and the Coalition of Nations alike.

Physically, Evrart is exceptionally corpulent, suffering from chronic gout and circulatory ailments that confine him to an oversized, custom-built ergonomic executive chair situated inside a retrofitted steel shipping container within the harbor terminal. Despite his physical infirmity and constant sweat, he radiates an aura of immense political intelligence, cheerful benevolence, and chilling Machiavellian cunning. He addresses visitors with exaggerated warmth, offering pleasantries, hot chicory coffee, and comfortable seating while subtly trapping them in intricate webs of patronage, legal blackmail, and political compromise.

Evrart operates as a champion of the Revacholian working class, funding neighborhood youth centers, retirement funds, and soup kitchens through union revenues. However, his methods are ruthlessly pragmatic, employing union enforcers, kickbacks, and strategic smuggling operations through Capeside to expand his dominion. He possesses detailed blueprints for commercial redevelopment and public housing across Martinaise. During the dockworkers strike, Evrart demonstrates brilliant strategic patience, leveraging the union's total control over the cargo gantry cranes to force the Wild Pines Group to negotiate on his terms, determined to transform Martinaise into an autonomous, union-controlled socialist fiefdom.
