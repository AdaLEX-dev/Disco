Title:
Klaasje Amandou

Text:
Klaasje Amandou, residing under the alias Miss Oranje Disco Dancer in Room 3 of the Whirling-in-Rags, is an elusive corporate fugitive whose presence serves as the catalyst for the murder investigation in Martinaise. Born in the Republic of Oranje, she previously worked as an operative specializing in industrial corporate espionage. After leaking highly sensitive financial intelligence that resulted in the catastrophic ruin of an offshore bank, she fled across the isolas, constantly evading private corporate security forces and contract hitmen. Taking refuge in the lawless post industrial enclave of Martinaise, she rented a modest room upstairs at the Whirling-in-Rags and adopted an unassuming party girl persona to blend into the coastal nightlife.

Klaasje entered into a volatile, drug fueled sexual liaison with Colonel Ellis Kortenaer, the commander of the Krenel private military detachment sent to break the local dock strike. When a high caliber sniper bullet struck Kortenaer through her hostel window, killing him instantly, Klaasje was thrust into mortal danger. Knowing that an official police inquiry into a corporate mercenary commander would expose her real identity to her pursuers, she orchestrated a sophisticated cover up. Enlisting the aid of the local dockworkers union and the Hardie boys, she had Kortenaer's corpse moved to the courtyard tree and staged as a retaliatory lynching.

Armed with extraordinary intelligence, tactical poise, and calculating psychological insight, Klaasje navigates police interrogations through carefully rehearsed vulnerability and deceptive misdirection. She relies on alcohol, tobacco, and prescription narcotics to stave off relentless paranoia, knowing that her survival hinges upon staying one step ahead of both municipal authorities and international corporate assassins.
