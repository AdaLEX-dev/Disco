Title:
Iosef Lilianovich Dros the Deserter

Text:
Iosef Lilianovich Dros, known historically as the Deserter, is an eighty year old former political commissar and sniper of the International Red Army who has lived in absolute seclusion for over four decades on the abandoned Sea Fortress island in the Bay of Revachol. Following the collapse of the Revachol Commune under Coalition naval bombardment in '08, Dros abandoned his comrades and retreated to the fortified sea ruins. From his concrete bunker, he has spent forty four years observing the mainland through high magnification sniper optics, watching the gradual restoration of bourgeois commerce and foreign colonial occupation with consuming ideological fury.

Dros survived on scavenged tinned rations, coastal fishing, and bitter revolutionary dogmatism, considering himself the sole uncorrupted communard soldier remaining on the Insulindian Isola. His intense isolation was complicated by his nesting proximity to an adult Insulindian Phasmid inhabiting the island's coastal reeds. Prolonged biochemical exposure to the creature's psychotropic pheromones accelerated his mental degeneration, fusing his Marxist political bitterness with obsessive sexual voyeurism directed at the young women of Martinaise, particularly Klaasje Amandou.

Driven by a toxic blend of ideological vengeance, sexual jealousy, and senile dementia, Dros pointed his antique military rifle toward the hostel window and fired the shot that killed mercenary colonel Ellis Kortenaer. His single act of bitter hatred ignited the industrial strike crisis, mercenary standoff, and subsequent bloodbath in Martinaise. When finally apprehended in the dark tunnels of the sea fort, Dros represents the tragic, venomous ghost of a crushed revolution, clutching a rusted rifle while the world he fought for recedes forever into the sea.
