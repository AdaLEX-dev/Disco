Title:
Cuno and Cunoesse

Text:
Kuuno de Ruyter, known throughout the district as Cuno, together with his lookout partner Cunoesse, is one of the most vibrant and unmistakable personalities inhabiting the coastal enclave of Martinaise. Cuno operates as the self-appointed guardian of the industrial courtyards and corrugated sheds situated behind the Whirling-in-Rags hostel. Defined by his kinetic energy, rapid delivery, and signature habit of speaking about himself exclusively in the third person, he approaches every visitor with vocal bravado and intense territorial pride. He spends his days traversing the district perimeter, scouting vacant architectural sites, and asserting his presence across the post-industrial landscape.

Stationed on the roof of an adjacent workshop shed is Cunoesse, his sharp-eyed companion. From her elevated vantage point, she monitors the surrounding alleyways, echoing Cuno's declarations and maintaining a vigilant watch over their shared territory. When Citizens Militia investigators arrive in Martinaise to conduct official inquiries, the duo greets them with relentless theatrical mockery, testing the detectives' patience from behind the courtyard fences and treating standard municipal authority with playful defiance.

Beneath this boastful and theatrical exterior lies an exceptionally perceptive observer of coastal life. Having spent years navigating every corner of Martinaise, Cuno possesses unmatched familiarity with local geography, including concealed passages through harbor fencing, union shift changes, and neighborhood rumors. He possesses genuine creative talent in large-scale spray painting and demonstrates an acute intuitive grasp of acoustic resonance within industrial ruins. When approached with calm professional seriousness and genuine respect, Cuno sets aside his defensive swagger, proving to be an insightful scout capable of providing vital observational details to precinct detectives operating along the Insulindian coast.
