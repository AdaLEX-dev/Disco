Title:
Garte the Cafeteria Manager

Text:
Garte serves as the long suffering day and night manager of the Whirling-in-Rags cafeteria and hostel in Martinaise, overseeing daily hospitality operations on behalf of an absentee corporate proprietor. Meticulous, pedantic, and perpetually high strung, he approaches his managerial duties with severe professional anxiety, attempting to maintain bourgeois commercial decorum in an impoverished post industrial district gripped by an indefinite dock strike. His routine consists of balancing ledger accounts, serving mediocre food and alcohol, handling difficult local clientele, and preventing violence between union dockworkers, corporate contractors, and vagrants.

His patience reaches complete exhaustion following the arrival of the Citizens Militia investigative detachment sent to Martinaise. Prior to the official commencement of the murder inquiry, an arriving RCM officer engaged in a catastrophic, narcotic fueled binge that resulted in immense structural damage across the hostel. Garte was left to document shattered windows, destroyed vintage taxidermy, wrecked furniture, and hundreds of real in unpaid bar tabs. He greets municipal authorities with open hostility and demands for monetary restitution, refusing to yield on administrative protocols or hotel bills regardless of homicide inquiries.

Despite his rigid exterior and obsessive insistence on financial accountability, Garte is an ordinary civilian trapped between terrifying regional powers. He fears the retribution of the Debardeurs Union, distrusts armed corporate mercenaries, and harbors no illusions regarding the institutional efficacy of the precinct militia. His encyclopedic memory regarding hostel guests, patron disputes, and courtyard activities makes him an invaluable source of logistical intelligence. Garte remains an enduring archetype of Revacholian petty bureaucracy, stubbornly demanding payment for broken glassware while historical catastrophes unfold around his front steps.
