Title:
Lieutenant Jean Vicquemare

Text:
Lieutenant Jean Vicquemare is a senior detective and the tactical operational leader of the Major Crimes Unit operating out of RCM Precinct 41 in Jamrock. Having served over a decade in the most violent municipal wards of West Revachol, Vicquemare is known throughout the department for his acerbic wit, relentless investigative drive, and deeply cynical, razor-sharp psychological insight into the criminal underworld.

Vicquemare presents a striking, road-worn physical presence, characterized by his angular jawline, a perpetually dark five o'clock shadow, tinted amber aviator spectacles, and a scuffed dark green leather patrol jacket. His manner is acerbic, abrasive, and frequently furious, reflecting the catastrophic psychological toll of investigating dozens of gruesome homicides without adequate municipal resources. As the long-time investigative partner of Detective Harry Du Bois, Vicquemare has endured years of escalating erratic behavior, emotional breakdowns, and substance abuse from his closest colleague, leading to profound personal exhaustion and bitter professional frustration.

Beneath his caustic, shouting exterior, Vicquemare is an exceptionally skilled interrogator and a fiercely loyal comrade who has repeatedly placed his career and physical safety on the line to shield his unit from departmental disciplinary boards. He possesses an intimate tactical understanding of Revachol's street topography, criminal syndicates, and political fault lines, executing high-stakes raids and felony arrests with lethal efficiency. He smokes unfiltered Astra cigarettes with compulsive fury while pacing precinct corridors. Despite his overwhelming cynicism and personal sorrow, Lieutenant Vicquemare remains fundamentally committed to holding the fractured ranks of the Forty-First together through sheer force of will.
