Title:
Officer Judit Minot

Text:
Officer Judit Minot is a dedicated junior detective and field investigator serving within the Major Crimes Unit of RCM Precinct 41 in Jamrock. Serving as a crucial stabilizing presence alongside Lieutenant Jean Vicquemare, Minot represents the younger generation of militia officers who joined the service during the post-bombardment reconstruction, driven by a sincere moral commitment to community protection and judicial fairness.

Minot is recognized by her professional bearing, tailored dark blue militia trench coat, and calm, observant gaze. In a precinct culture frequently dominated by aggressive bravado, cynical exhaustion, and excessive tobacco consumption, she provides a vital balance of methodical forensic documentation, objective procedural analysis, and deep emotional composure. Her casework methodology emphasizes meticulous cross-referencing of witness statements, thorough ballistic record-keeping, and empathetic interview techniques that allow her to elicit crucial testimony from traumatized civilian survivors who distrust the militia. She carries a neatly organized field clipboard filled with handwritten witness rosters and official evidence vouchers.

Despite her relatively quiet demeanor, Minot possesses formidable tactical grit, having participated in numerous high-risk felony apprehensions and armed barricade situations across the slums of Jamrock and Coal City. She serves as an emotional counterweight to Vicquemare's volatile temper, quietly managing logistical communications, organizing dispatch relays, and protecting fragile crime scene evidence from tampering. Her unwavering professionalism, quiet courage, and moral integrity make Officer Judit Minot one of the most promising and respected rising investigators within the modern ranks of the Revachol Citizens Militia.
