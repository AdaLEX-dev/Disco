Title:
Captain Pryce

Text:
Captain Ptolemy Pryce is the commanding officer of Precinct 41 of the Revachol Citizens Militia, headquartered in the Jamrock district of West Revachol. A weathered, battle-hardened veteran who survived both the Antecentennial War and the violent street fighting of the Revolution of '02, Pryce is widely regarded as one of the most formidable, politically astute, and fiercely protective precinct commanders serving within the militia's fractured municipal structure.

Physically imposing with grey-streaked hair, broad shoulders, and a perpetually weary gaze, Pryce directs the operations of the Forty-First from a cramped, smoke-filled corner office overlooking the central bullpen. Unlike junior commanders who bend to the bureaucratic demands of the foreign Coalition of Nations, Pryce maintains a fierce institutional independence, treating Coalition administrative liaisons with polite contempt while quietly safeguarding his detectives from external interference. He understands the precarious nature of the peace holding Revachol together and operates with the grim realization that a secondary civil uprising or armed intervention remains an ever-present possibility.

Pryce's leadership style combines uncompromising operational realism with deep personal loyalty toward his subordinate officers. He grants his senior detectives extraordinary tactical autonomy to resolve high-profile homicides across hostile gang territories, while personally managing the municipal budget deficits, supply shortages, and political fallout generated by their unorthodox field methods. A battered tin mug of chicory coffee rests perpetually on his desk beside classified precinct emergency contingency binders. Behind his cynical exterior lies a profound commitment to the survival of the Revacholian working class, making Captain Pryce the indispensable institutional shield protecting the fragile integrity of the militia.
