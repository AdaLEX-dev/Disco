Title:
Lieutenant Kim Kitsuragi

Text:
Lieutenant Kim Kitsuragi is a senior investigative detective in the Revachol Citizens Militia, assigned to Precinct 57 in the dense commercial quarter of Central Jamrock. Born and raised in Revachol of Seolite descent, Kitsuragi embodies the disciplined, unyielding professionalism required of an officer navigating a deeply divided post-revolutionary metropolis. Over fifteen years of service in the militia, he has earned a stellar departmental reputation for methodological precision, unimpeachable integrity, and an exceptional clearance rate in difficult urban homicides.

Visually, Kitsuragi is distinguished by his lean physical frame, neat prescription spectacles, and his signature bright orange high-collar flight jacket, beneath which he wears dark cargo trousers and reinforced patrol boots. His demeanor is composed, stoic, and remarkably patient, maintaining strict procedural discipline even when confronted by hostile union dockworkers, violent street gangs, or unstable fellow officers. While fully conscious of the systemic corruption and racial prejudice that permeate Revacholian society, Kitsuragi consistently refuses ideological cynicism, choosing instead to execute his sworn civic duties with meticulous, quiet dedication.

Kitsuragi operates a heavily customized Coupris Kineema motor carriage, which he maintains personally with passionate mechanical expertise. His investigative technique relies on rigorous sensory observation, forensic evidence collection, and detailed field notes recorded in a pocket notebook using neat, condensed shorthand. A quiet enthusiast of mechanical engineering and regional aerostat racing, he permits himself precisely one Astra cigarette per day at sunset. Within the precarious jurisdiction of West Revachol, Lieutenant Kitsuragi serves as a vital stabilizing anchor, balancing deductive brilliance with human empathy in the pursuit of objective truth.
