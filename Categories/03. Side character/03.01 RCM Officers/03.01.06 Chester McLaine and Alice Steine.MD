Title:
Chester McLaine and Alice Steine

Text:
Chester McLaine and Alice Steine constitute a formidable investigative and tactical patrol team operating within RCM Precinct 41 in the Jamrock district of West Revachol. Serving as frontline field officers, the pair handles high-volume felony dispatches, street containment during riot situations, and preliminary homicide response, representing the gritty, unglamorous backbone of the precinct's daily law enforcement operations.

Chester McLaine is a boisterous, heavyset plainclothes detective known throughout the precinct for his booming voice, crude humor, and flamboyant floral print shirts worn beneath a scuffed leather jacket. A former alley brawler who grew up in the industrial wards of Coal City, McLaine relies on raw physical intimidation, street-level informants, and instinctual bravado to extract confessions from petty thieves and dockland enforcers. Despite his clownish exterior and cynical jokes, McLaine is a fiercely loyal partner who has survived numerous shootouts and knife encounters across the Jamrock tenements.

Alice Steine, by contrast, is a sharp-eyed, procedural patrolwoman characterized by her practical demeanor, short-cropped hair, and immaculate uniform maintenance. Steine excels at logistical coordination, perimeter defense, and radio dispatch protocols, keeping her volatile partner grounded during chaotic street skirmishes. She maintains precise logbooks of precinct vehicle mileage and radio call signs with unbending discipline. Operating together from a battered municipal patrol carriage, McLaine and Steine provide essential tactical containment, evidence transport, and crowd control during major homicide inquiries, standing as an indispensable pair of working-class cops committed to surviving the unforgiving streets of Revachol West amidst the rain.
