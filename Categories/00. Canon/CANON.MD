# Canon and Source Authority: Insulindian Isola & Revachol

This document defines the strict canonical authority for the Disco Elysium Lorebook. The scope of this project is strictly confined to the Insulindian Isola and the city of Revachol, capped at a maximum of 128 comprehensive entries for direct integration into Character.ai.

## Geographic and Narrative Scope
1. Strictly Insulindian Isola:
   - All entries must pertain directly to the Insulindian Isola, the Bay of Revachol, the city of Revachol, its districts (Martinaise, Jamrock, Coal City, Old Town, Grand-Couron, Stella Maris, etc.), its inhabitants, factions, items, systems, culture, and creatures.
   - Do not flesh out external isolas (Mundi, Graad, Seol, Katla, Samara, etc.) as standalone entries. External regions may only be referenced in passing when providing origin context for immigrants, trade goods, or foreign Coalition forces physically operating in Revachol.
2. Capacity Ceiling:
   - Maximum 128 total markdown entry files across categories 01 through 09.
   - Every entry is written with exhaustive detail, depth, and fidelity to in-game dialogue and primary texts.

## Tier 1 Primary Canon
1. Disco Elysium: The Final Cut
   - Master in-game dialogue database (1.2+ million words, over 67,000 dialogue nodes).
   - In-game environmental descriptions, thought cabinet texts, item lore, and audio transcripts.
2. Sacred and Terrible Air (Puha ja oudne lohn) by Robert Kurvitz (2013)
   - Official novel set in the Elysium universe covering the Insulindian timeline, the fate of Revachol, deep Pale mechanics, and the Great Disappearance.

## Tier 2 Supporting Canon
1. Official ZA/UM Worldbuilding Design Documents and Dev Blogs (2016-2021)
   - Essays by Robert Kurvitz, Helen Hindpere, and Argo Tuulik on Revacholian sociology, history, and the Antecentennial War.
2. Developer Interviews on Revacholian Mechanics
   - Authoritative developer clarifications on RCM history, aerostatics, filament wire radiocomputing, and Insulindian pale-boundaries.

## Explicitly Non-Canonical (Strictly Prohibited)
- Fanfiction, unverified forum conjectures, fan roleplay logs, and third-party wiki speculation not corroborated by game scripts.
- Invented characters, speculative future timelines outside Kurvitz's novel, or artificial worldbuilding outside the Insulindian Isola.
