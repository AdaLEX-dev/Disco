# Canon and Source Authority

This document defines the strict canonical authority for the Disco Elysium Lorebook. All entries must draw exclusively from these materials. No outside speculation, fanfiction, or unauthorized headcanons are permitted.

## Tier 1 Primary Canon
1. Disco Elysium: The Final Cut
   - Complete in-game dialogue trees, descriptions, item texts, thought cabinet entries, and internal monologues.
   - Master dialogue database (1.2+ million words, over 67,000 dialogue nodes).
   - Voice recordings, soundscape transcripts, and in-game environmental texts.
2. Sacred and Terrible Air (Puha ja oudne lohn) by Robert Kurvitz (2013)
   - Official novel set in the Elysium universe set decades after the events of Disco Elysium.
   - Provides canonical world mechanics regarding the Pale, isolas, history of Perikarnasis, and geopolitical fates.

## Tier 2 Supporting Canon
1. Official ZA/UM Worldbuilding Design Documents and Dev Blogs (2016-2021)
   - World history essays written by Robert Kurvitz, Helen Hindpere, and Argo Tuulik.
   - Official artbook commentary and concept descriptions.
2. Developer Interviews regarding world mechanics
   - Specific statements on physics, Pale traversal, motorics, and historical timelines.

## Explicitly Non-Canonical (Strictly Prohibited)
- Fanfiction, unverified forum claims, roleplay logs, and third-party wiki conjectures not backed by in-game quotes.
- Hallucinations or invented historical events, dates, characters, or mechanics.
