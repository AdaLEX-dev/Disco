Title:
Sunday Friends and Bureaucrats

Text:
The Sunday Friends and Bureaucrats represent the clandestine, elite technocratic network of foreign diplomats, financial commissioners, and high ranking administrators who manage the political and economic affairs of the Coalition Administrative Region. Deriving their informal moniker from their habitual practice of convening private weekend luncheons in exclusive foreign embassies, luxury hotels, and private maritime yachts anchored in the Bay of Revachol, these foreign functionaries operate the real levers of administrative power across the Insulindian Isola with polite, chilling detachment.

Drawn from the premier diplomatic corps and banking houses of dominant Moralintern nations such as Mundi and Graad, the Sunday Friends are tasked with maintaining regional macroeconomic stability, enforcing foreign debt repayments, and supervising commercial trade deregulations. They view Revachol not as a historic society of living human beings, but as a complex logistical problem and an experimental colonial territory to be rationally administered. Seated in comfortable, air conditioned salons far removed from the stench and poverty of Jamrock, they approve budget reductions, municipal austerity programs, and police allocations over fine wine and delicate pastries.

Their authority is absolute yet deliberately obscured behind dense layers of international treaties, multilateral councils, and corporate maritime concessions. By ensuring that local municipal institutions remain fragmented and financially dependent upon foreign loans, the Sunday Friends prevent the emergence of genuine democratic sovereignty in Revachol. They embody the smiling, bloodless face of modern global governance, maintaining imperial dominion through legal contracts, economic pressure, and administrative indifference while ordinary citizens bear the crushing burden of post war decay.
