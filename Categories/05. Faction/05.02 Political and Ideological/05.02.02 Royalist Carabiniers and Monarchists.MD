Title:
Royalist Carabiniers and Monarchists

Text:
The Royalist Carabiniers and Monarchists represent the conservative, reactionary political faction dedicated to preserving the memory, aristocratic hierarchy, and imperial glory of the fallen Frissel royal dynasty. Prior to the revolution of Year '02, the royalist establishment commanded absolute sovereign authority across Revachol, centered at the opulent palatial court on Le Caillou. The military vanguard of monarchic power was the King's Carabinier regiment, an elite military corps renowned for their resplendent blue and gold uniforms, polished cavalry sabers, and fanatical devotion to the divine person of King Frissel.

During the outbreak of the Revachol Commune, the Carabiniers fought with unyielding ferocity across the barricades, executing summary executions of captured socialist partisans before being overwhelmed by the armed proletariat and mutinous naval detachments. When foreign Coalition forces invaded the city in '08, surviving royalist elements collaborated eagerly with the foreign invaders, assisting occupation forces in identifying and executing communard leaders in a desperate, ultimately futile bid to restore the monarchic throne. To their bitter disillusionment, the victorious Coalition discarded the royalists, instituting a capitalist administrative protectorate rather than restoring the crown.

In contemporary Revachol, the monarchist movement is reduced to an aging, impoverished subculture of disenfranchised aristocrats, embittered military veterans, and conservative social clubs. Stripped of ancestral estates and political power, royalists gather in private salons and public parks, bitterly reminiscing over forgotten imperial triumphs while wearing faded medals and tarnished carabinier jackets. Despising both foreign Moralist bureaucrats and working class trade unionists with equal vehemence, the monarchists endure as proud, melancholy ghosts of a magnificent, vanished empire that will never rise again.
