Title:
Revacholian Nationalists

Text:
The Revacholian Nationalists represent a fragmented yet vocal political and cultural faction driven by reactionary grievance, anti-immigrant sentiment, and bitter nostalgia for pre-revolutionary sovereign grandeur. Disillusioned by decades of foreign military occupation, economic disenfranchisement, and cultural erosion under the Coalition Administrative Region, nationalist sympathizers can be found across the working class districts of Revachol West, Jamrock, and Coal City. United primarily by their fierce opposition to foreign control, they view the international Moralintern and its corporate sponsors as predatory colonial conquerors that reduced the imperial capital of Elysium to an impoverished, divided protectorate.

Lacking a unified political party, the movement manifests as localized patriotic leagues, veterans fraternal orders, and informal street associations. Their ideology is heavily inflected by revanchist mythology, idealizing an uncorrupted historical Revachol that was betrayed from within and without. Instead of directing their anger toward the foreign corporate monopolies that extract regional wealth, nationalist discourse frequently targets vulnerable immigrant communities, directing venomous rhetoric toward Seolite immigrants, Semenese workers, and Graad political refugees whom they blame for driving down wages, altering local cultural heritage, and introducing urban decay.

Prominent figures within the nationalist sphere range from embittered lorry drivers stranded by harbour strikes to eccentric racial theoreticians operating in the docklands. While municipal authorities and the Debardeurs Union view nationalist rhetoric with disdain, the movement taps into deep reservoirs of popular resentment among an impoverished population yearning for lost pride. The Nationalists reflect the tragic pathology of a defeated civilization, attempting to reconstruct an imagined imperial identity from the hollow ruins of military humiliation.
