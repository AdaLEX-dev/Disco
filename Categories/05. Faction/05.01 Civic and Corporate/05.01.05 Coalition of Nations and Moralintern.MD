Title:
Coalition of Nations and Moralintern

Text:
The Coalition of Nations, guided ideologically by the doctrine of the Moralist International or Moralintern, is the supreme foreign military and political alliance that exercises sovereign occupational authority over the city of Revachol. Composed of dominant capitalist and moralist world powers centered primarily in the distant isolas of Mundi and Graad, the Coalition intervened militarily in Year '08 to violently extinguish the Revachol Commune, crushing the fledgling socialist republic under offshore naval bombardment and establishing an indefinite foreign protectorate over the Insulindian Isola.

Administering the territory through the bureaucratic apparatus of the Coalition Administrative Region, the Moralintern operates from fortified administrative compounds, luxury hotel suites, and secure coastal installations across the metropolis. Its political philosophy espouses incremental centrism, administrative rationality, free market commercial access, and the absolute containment of revolutionary radicalism. Under Coalition rule, Revachol is deliberately denied national sovereignty, democratic self government, or autonomous fiscal policy, effectively transforming the historic imperial capital into a deregulated offshore commercial enclave optimized for foreign corporate exploitation.

The Moralintern exercises its authority through detached diplomatic envoys, foreign financial advisors, and shadowy intelligence networks known casually among locals as the Sunday Friends. While publicly claiming to provide stability, international investment, and gradual reconstruction to a devastated population, the Coalition maintains an uncompromising grip on civic order, quietly prepared to deploy overwhelming military force against any domestic uprising that threatens to disrupt the geopolitical equilibrium of the southern hemisphere.

Keywords:
Coalition of Nations, Moralintern, Moralism, occupation, Administrative Region, Sunday Friends
