Title:
Debardeurs Union Local 41

Text:
The Debardeurs Union Local 41 is the immense, militant maritime labor organization that commands total operational control over the Martinaise Commercial Harbour and its surrounding industrial infrastructure. Originally formed as a mutual aid brotherhood among cargo handlers, crane operators, and stevedores during the final decades of the Frissel monarchy, the union evolved into an indispensable economic powerhouse across Revachol West. Governed with autocratic populist mastery by its portly union boss Evrart Claire and his twin brother Edgar, Local 41 operates as a self sufficient shadow municipality, administering local welfare, private healthcare clinics, public works, and territorial security independently of Coalition authority.

The union derives its extraordinary political power from its complete monopoly over Insulindian maritime logistics. Martinaise serves as the primary deep water portal through which manufactured goods, raw timber, and essential consumer commodities enter the western districts. By mobilizing thousands of disciplined dockworkers to execute general strikes and gate lockouts, the union can instantaneously paralyze commerce across the entire Bay of Revachol, inflicting catastrophic financial losses upon international shipping conglomerates and challenging the political supremacy of the Coalition Administrative Region.

While widely revered by the local working class as the sole institutional champion of Insulindian sovereignty against foreign corporate exploitation, Local 41 operates with ruthless pragmatic ambition. The union maintains an extensive network of political patronage, engages in clandestine gray market logistics, and utilizes its loyal dockland council to enforce community conformity. Balancing genuine working class solidarity with calculated territorial expansion, the Debardeurs Union represents the most formidable, organized domestic counterweight to foreign capitalist dominance in post-war Revachol.
