Title:
The Hardie Boys Council

Text:
The Hardie Boys Council functions as the informal vigilante peacekeeping corps, strike defense detachment, and neighborhood tribunal of the Debardeurs Union in Martinaise. Headquartered inside the ground floor cafeteria of the Whirling-in-Rags hostel, where they occupy a central dining booth, the council is commanded by senior union shop steward Titus Hardie. Composed of veteran dockworkers, mechanical technicians, and cargo handlers including Alain, Eugene, Theo, Shanky, and the muscular twins Angus and Fat Angus, the group serves as the immediate civilian law of the district in the historic absence of regular Citizens Militia patrols.

The council arose out of acute communal necessity following the collapse of municipal administration in Revachol West. Rather than permitting neighborhood disputes, theft, and domestic violence to fester unaddressed in an abandoned industrial enclave, the Hardie boys established a rough, localized system of working class justice. They settle tenant conflicts, regulate black market commerce, protect union families from predatory outside criminals, and enforce picket line discipline with heavy fists, baseball bats, and hidden hunting rifles. Their authority is recognized and respected by the local population, who view them as authentic neighbors rather than hostile occupational agents.

Beneath their gruff, combative exterior and frequent posturing over cafeteria beers, the council members are bound by fierce collective loyalty and genuine civic pride. When an international corporate mercenary commander is found murdered in the hostel yard, the Hardie boys close ranks to shield the community from foreign military retaliation, presenting a unified front of defiant solidarity. While their methods are often legally irregular and prone to violent escalation, the council embodies the rugged communal autonomy of the Martinaise dockworkers, standing resolute between defenseless civilians and encroaching corporate violence.
