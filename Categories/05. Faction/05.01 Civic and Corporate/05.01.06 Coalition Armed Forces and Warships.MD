Title:
Coalition Armed Forces and Warships

Text:
The Coalition Armed Forces and Warships represent the overwhelming, technologically supreme military apparatus that enforces the geopolitical subjugation of Revachol and the Insulindian Isola. Following the naval invasion of Year '08, the Moralintern established a permanent military garrison designed to project devastating kinetic power across the Bay of Revachol. The foundation of this occupying force consists of a heavily armored naval armada stationed in the outer roadsteads, featuring massive ironclad dreadnoughts, coastal bombardment monitors, and automated missile cruisers capable of leveling coastal neighborhoods within minutes of receiving operational orders.

Complementing this naval armada is the Coalition air fleet, spearheaded by the colossal aerostat battle cruiser Archer, which patrols the skies above the Insulindian coastline with silent, menacing permanence. The Coalition maintains specialized motorized infantry garrisons and rapid deployment strike units quartered in secure extraterritorial military zones on Le Caillou and northern coastal bastions. These foreign combat formations are equipped with state of the art compression rifles, motorized infantry carriages, advanced radar radiocomputers, and heavy ballistic armor, far outclassing the obsolete civilian firearms possessed by local citizens or municipal militia officers.

The primary strategic doctrine of the Coalition military is not everyday civil peacekeeping, but the ruthless preservation of imperial hegemony and the defense of international shipping lanes. Ordinary street crime, gang conflicts, and domestic homicides are deliberately ignored as municipal trivialities beneath imperial notice. However, should urban unrest threaten corporate infrastructure or manifest as organized anti-colonial rebellion, the Coalition military stands ready to unleash catastrophic aerial and naval devastation, serving as the ultimate, unyielding iron fist behind Moralist governance in Revachol.
