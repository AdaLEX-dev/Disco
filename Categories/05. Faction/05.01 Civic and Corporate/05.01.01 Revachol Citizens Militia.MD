Title:
Revachol Citizens Militia

Text:
The Revachol Citizens Militia stands as the sole functioning municipal peacekeeping and criminal investigative agency operating across the ruined urban sprawl of Revachol. Established in the aftermath of the failed Revachol Commune and the subsequent Coalition invasion, the militia grew out of grassroots neighborhood defense committees, volunteer watch brigades, and demobilized revolutionary veterans who stepped forward to curb the rampant lawlessness that engulfed the shattered city. The occupying Moralintern authorities granted the volunteer organization reluctant recognition, utilizing the militia as an inexpensive domestic buffer to maintain civil peace without committing foreign troops to routine municipal policing.

Operating under severe jurisdictional and material limitations imposed by the Coalition Administrative Region, the RCM is explicitly barred from maintaining heavy military hardware, automatic weaponry, or dedicated aerial assets. Sworn officers navigate an impoverished institutional existence, relying on aging motor carriages, dilapidated precinct stations, and meager municipal stipends. The organization is decentralized across autonomous regional stations, most notably Precinct 41 in Jamrock and Precinct 57 in Couron, each developing distinct institutional cultures ranging from hard bitten detective squads to weary neighborhood beat patrols.

Despite enduring persistent underfunding, chronic bureaucratic neglect, and deep public cynicism from citizens who view them as tools of foreign occupation, the RCM performs an essential, harrowing civic duty. Its investigators and patrolmen confront brutal homicides, organized dockland smuggling, and violent street disputes with pragmatic resolve. Grounded in street level familiarity and moral endurance rather than statutory authority, the RCM remains the fragile, battered thin line preventing the fragmented districts of the Insulindian metropolis from collapsing into total societal dissolution.
