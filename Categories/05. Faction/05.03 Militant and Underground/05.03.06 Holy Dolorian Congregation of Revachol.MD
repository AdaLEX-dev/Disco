Title:
Holy Dolorian Congregation of Revachol

Text:
The Holy Dolorian Congregation of Revachol is the historic spiritual institution and religious establishment dedicated to the veneration of Innocence Dolores Dei, whose divine arrival over three centuries ago founded modern Insulindian civilization. Established as the supreme moral and religious authority during the royalist era, the church constructed monumental stone basilicas, elaborate stained glass cathedral domes, and monastic welfare institutions across the capital, embedding Dolorian theology, heraldry, and saintly iconography into the deepest cultural architecture of Revachol.

Following the revolutionary overthrow of the monarchy and the subsequent secularization instituted by the Commune, the power of the church suffered catastrophic decline. During the Coalition bombardment of Year '08, numerous historic church structures were severely damaged or reduced to rubble by naval artillery fire. In contemporary Revachol, the congregation exists as a melancholy shadow of its former imperial glory, presiding over crumbling sanctuaries with dwindling, elderly congregations and struggling to maintain its vast architectural holdings in impoverished districts like Martinaise.

Despite its diminished political authority, the Dolorian faith retains a quiet, resilient hold over the spiritual imagination of many ordinary citizens. In times of acute personal grief, industrial disaster, or fear of the encroaching pale, residents continue to light votive candles before tarnished stained glass portraits of Dolores Dei, seeking solace in her radiant countenance. Preserving ancient religious liturgies, sacred choral music, and historic colonial records, the congregation endures as a solemn, dignified relic of divine hope amidst the post-industrial exhaustion of the Insulindian Isola.
