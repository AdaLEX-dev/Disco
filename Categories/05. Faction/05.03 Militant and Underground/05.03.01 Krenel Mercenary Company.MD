Title:
Krenel Mercenary Company

Text:
The Krenel Mercenary Company is a notorious, heavily armed private military contractor originating from the Republic of Graad, renowned across Elysium for its ruthless efficiency, technological sophistication, and brutal operational methods. Composed primarily of combat veterans from vicious colonial pacification campaigns in the eastern isolas, Krenel provides specialized security enforcement, tactical strike capabilities, and industrial counter-insurgency services to multinational corporate cartels seeking to protect private assets beyond the effective reach of international law.

Hired secretly by the executive board of the Wild Pines Group to break the paralyzing Debardeurs Union strike in Martinaise, an elite Krenel tactical detachment was deployed under the command of Colonel Ellis Kortenaer, accompanied by his sadistic subordinates Raul de Pauw and the psychotic heavy machine gunner Ruud Hoogenstraten. Equipped with state of the art Fairweather T-56 composite ceramic armor, high velocity compression rifles, and advanced combat stimulants, the mercenaries arrived in the district expecting an easy, violent suppression of defenseless dockworkers, operating with arrogant impunity.

The covert deployment rapidly disintegrated into a bloody disaster following the sniper assassination of Colonel Kortenaer. Robbed of professional command and consumed by alcohol, grief, and chemical combat psychosis, the surviving mercenaries abandoned all corporate discipline, transforming into an unhinged armed lynch mob bent on collective retribution. Their violent confrontation with Titus Hardie and the union dockworkers resulted in the catastrophic Martinaise mercenary tribunal, exposing the terrifying moral depravity of privatized imperial violence unleashed upon a defenseless civilian population.
