Title:
The Isola of Samara

Text:
Samara is a densely populated and historically contentious isola situated across the pale from Graad and Mundi, characterized by temperate agricultural river valleys, sprawling industrial metropolises, and rugged inland plateaus. Long dominated by a coalition of socialist people republics, Samara represents a major bastion of organized labor, planned state economics, and collective social engineering in the contemporary era. Its urban centers are characterized by vast monumental architecture, broad parade squares, and modular housing districts built to accommodate millions of industrial workers, engineers, and agronomists. Throughout the Century of Revolution, Samara provided crucial material, financial, and ideological backing to revolutionary movements across Insulinde, leading to lasting geopolitical hostility between Samaran leadership and the Moralist International. Today, Samara maintains a formidable military apparatus and extensive research infrastructure focusing on radiocomputing, heavy chemical synthesis, and aerostatic flight technology. Despite international economic sanctions and diplomatic isolation, Samara remains a self-sufficient industrial powerhouse with a deeply patriotic and ideologically disciplined civilian populace.
