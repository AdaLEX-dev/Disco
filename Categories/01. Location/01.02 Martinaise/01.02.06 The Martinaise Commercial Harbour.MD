Title:
The Martinaise Commercial Harbour

Text:
The Martinaise Commercial Harbour is a massive, heavily fortified maritime cargo terminal situated along the eastern shoreline of the Martinaise district in West Revachol. Operated under the direct administrative control of the Debardeurs Union Local 41, the harbor serves as the primary gateway for industrial container freight, heavy bulk machinery, and agricultural commodities entering the metropolitan markets of Le Caillou from foreign manufacturing centers across Elysium.

The facility comprises expansive deep-water concrete quays, automated rail sidings, and vast open storage yards filled with towering tiers of standardized steel shipping containers. Enormous electric gantry cranes and diesel container straddlers dominate the industrial skyline, loading and unloading international merchant vessels docked along the deep dredged channel. The main landward entrance is secured by a reinforced steel gate and security checkpoints manned day and night by armed union debardeurs, effectively barring municipal authorities and Coalition inspectors from entering without union permits. Picket lines and brazier barrels burning coal scrap often line the perimeter during labor conflicts.

Within the perimeter, administrative operations are directed from a cluster of retrofitted shipping containers that house the executive offices of Union Boss Evrart Claire. The harbor functions not merely as a commercial logistics hub, but as the geopolitical fortress of the Revacholian labor movement. During industrial disputes and general strikes, the union's complete control over the cargo cranes grants it decisive economic leverage, enabling it to paralyze commercial distribution across Greater Revachol and confront multinational corporate shipping conglomerates like the Wild Pines Group on equal terms.
