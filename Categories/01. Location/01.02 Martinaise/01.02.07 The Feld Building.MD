Title:
The Feld Building

Text:
The Feld Building is an immense, derelict industrial manufacturing and administrative complex occupying a prominent cliffside position on the western waterfront of Martinaise. Originally erected in the early twentieth century by the Feld Electrical company, the facility served as a premier center of technical innovation, pioneering the domestic development and assembly of magnetic tape radiocomputers, high-density filament storage wire, and advanced audio recording equipment for civilian and military markets across Insulinde.

The complex is built from heavy load-bearing red brick, reinforced concrete slabs, and industrial steel trusses. Its towering seaside facade, visible for miles across the Bay of Revachol, is famous for displaying a massive hand-painted revolutionary mural bearing the weathered slogan: SOMETHING BEAUTIFUL WILL HAPPEN. The interior contains sprawling multi-story assembly halls, abandoned electrical workshops, testing laboratories, and an intricate subterranean bomb shelter system constructed beneath the granite foundation to protect personnel from naval bombardment. Rusted magnetic tape ribbons, broken cathode terminals, and waterlogged schematic blueprints remain strewn across the tiled laboratory floors.

Following the bankruptcy of Feld Electrical during the collapse of the Antecentennial War and the subsequent destruction of the district during the Revolution of '02, the building was permanently abandoned. The roof has partially collapsed under decades of rain and sea spray, flooding the lower workshops and rusting the machinery into monolithic iron sculptures. A hidden natural sea cave connects the lowest basement vault directly to the outer rocky reef, providing a secret maritime egress known only to smugglers, fugitives, and forgotten snipers who navigate the dark waters of the bay.
