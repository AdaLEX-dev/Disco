Title:
Martinaise District

Text:
Martinaise is a coastal industrial district situated in western Revachol on the island of Le Caillou within the Insulindian Isola. Facing the cold waters of the Bay of Revachol, Martinaise was originally developed during the late nineteenth century as a seaside resort destination for the Revacholian aristocracy, featuring ornate timber pavilions, beachfront bathhouses, and private pleasure villas. Following the rapid expansion of maritime freight commerce and heavy industry, the district transitioned into a vital transshipment quarter dominated by commercial shipping wharves, cargo railyards, and working class tenements.

During the Antecentennial War and the Revolution of '02, Martinaise became a key battleground due to its strategic port infrastructure and proximity to the open bay. Communard forces fortified the district, utilizing its heavy brick warehouses as bastions. In the climactic Coalition intervention of '08, Coalition naval squadrons subjected the waterfront to relentless artillery shelling, obliterating entire blocks and reducing the pleasure promenade to ruins. When foreign troops landed, fierce street combat destroyed the remaining municipal utilities, leaving structural craters and shrapnel scars that remain unrepaired half a century later.

In the contemporary post-revolutionary era, Martinaise exists in an anomalous state of civic isolation and economic neglect. Administratively abandoned by the Coalition authorities and ignored by the municipal government, the district lacks an active police precinct and functions under the de facto control of the Debardeurs Union Local 41. The local population consists of dockworkers, seasonal crane operators, net-menders, and impoverished retirees enduring bitter coastal damp, economic hardship, and the tense fallout of industrial labor strikes.
