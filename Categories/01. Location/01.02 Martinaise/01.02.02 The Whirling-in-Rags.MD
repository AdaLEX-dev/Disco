Title:
The Whirling-in-Rags

Text:
The Whirling-in-Rags is a prominent multi-story commercial cafeteria and hostel establishment situated in the central urban square of the Martinaise district in West Revachol. Originally constructed during the late monarchy as a luxurious silk-canopied resort pavilion known as The Whirling Rag, the building catered to wealthy bourgeois vacationers patronizing the coastal beaches. Following the industrial decline of the seaside resort and the devastation of the Coalition bombardment, the structure was rebuilt with heavy brickwork, stucco plaster, and an orange-tiled pitched roof.

The ground floor of the establishment operates as a public cafeteria, tavern, and neighborhood gathering hall managed on a franchise lease by Garte. It features a long wooden service counter, an industrial espresso and chicory boiler, simple dining tables, and large street-facing glass windows overlooking the municipal roundabout. The establishment serves cheap, hearty meals, chicory coffee, local beers, and distilled spirits to dockworkers, transit drivers, and neighborhood residents. The upper floors house modest hostel guest rooms leased on daily or weekly terms, accessible via an interior timber staircase.

The building forms the eastern boundary of a larger architectural complex that incorporates the derelict Doomed Commercial Area. An interior service door behind the kitchen connects to forgotten basement corridors, while an upper floor opens onto a rooftop terrace offering panoramic views of the Martinaise waterfront, the cargo terminal, and the smoking chimneys of the district. In the modern era, the Whirling-in-Rags stands as the sole functioning public establishment offering lodging and warm food in central Martinaise, serving as an involuntary crossroads for visiting officials, union organizers, and travelers.
