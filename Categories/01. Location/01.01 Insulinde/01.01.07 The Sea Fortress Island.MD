Title:
The Sea Fortress Island

Text:
The Sea Fortress Island is a rugged, solitary granite outcropping situated in the Bay of Revachol approximately one nautical mile off the western coast of Martinaise. Occupying a commanding tactical position at the mouth of the harbor, the island was fortified over several centuries to defend the maritime approaches to Revachol. Its prominent natural height and sheer cliffs made it an ideal platform for heavy artillery installations designed to interdict hostile warships entering the bay.

During the Suzerainty of Revachol, the island housed imperial coastal batteries, subterranean powder magazines, and barracks for marine carabiniers. Following the outbreak of the Revolution of '02, the fortress was seized by Communard forces and converted into a key military bastion manned by the Red Army. In the final days of the conflict in '08, the fortress endured sustained naval bombardment from Coalition warships, which obliterated the upper stone ramparts, collapsed multiple subterranean bunker complexes, and forced the eventual surrender of the garrison.

Today, the Sea Fortress Island stands largely abandoned and derelict, its concrete gun casemates and shattered masonry exposed to the relentless salt winds and sea spray. The collapsed tunnels and flooded lower galleries remain perilous, strewn with rusted artillery fragments, structural debris, and unexploded munitions. Despite its ruined state, the island retains an imposing, somber silhouette visible from the Martinaise boardwalk, serving as a silent physical monument to the catastrophic violence of the Coalition intervention. Local legends among Martinaise fishermen claim that strange optical anomalies, eerie echoes, and phantom radio signals still drift from the subterranean ruins on freezing winter nights, carrying echoes of a dead revolution.
