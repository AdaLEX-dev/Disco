Title:
Sea of Insulinde

Text:
The Sea of Insulinde is the vast oceanic expanse that encompasses the entire Insulindian Isola, encircling Le Caillou and extending to the outer atmospheric boundaries where physical reality dissolves into the pale. Comprising millions of square kilometers of open water, it represents one of the largest bodies of navigable salt water known in the cosmography of Elysium. Its currents flow in complex circular gyres influenced by seasonal atmospheric variations and the subtle gravitational distortions associated with interisolary pale borders.

Navigation across the Sea of Insulinde forms the lifeline of the isola. Commercial cargo vessels, passenger liners, and military warships rely on heavily charted deep-water corridors to connect Revachol with outlying archipelagos and the aerostat terminals that bridge the pale toward Mundi and Graad. The weather on the open sea is notoriously volatile, characterized by sudden gales, freezing squalls, and dense oceanic fog banks that can obscure navigation for days at a time. Maritime safety is further complicated by shallow reef structures and unexploded ordnance dating to the Antecentennial War.

Marine life within the Sea of Insulinde remains rich despite centuries of intense commercial trawling and industrial pollution near the urban estuaries. Deep water species include cold-water cod, migratory sea-bears, giant squid, and various species of armored benthic crustaceans. Independent fishing communities scattered along the coastlines of Le Caillou and minor islets depend entirely on seasonal fish migrations, navigating the swells in wooden trawlers equipped with auxiliary diesel motors. Marine salvage operators frequently patrol the coastal shallows to recover abandoned freight containers and adrift timber cargo lost during turbulent winter crossings.
