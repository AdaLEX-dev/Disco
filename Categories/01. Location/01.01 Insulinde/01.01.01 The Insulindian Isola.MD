Title:
The Insulindian Isola

Text:
The Insulindian Isola is a vast oceanic territory situated within the known world of Elysium. Discovered approximately three hundred and eighty years ago during the historical westward voyage initiated by Innocence Dolores Dei, the isola is characterized by its overwhelming proportion of open salt water interrupted by the continental landmass of Le Caillou and numerous surrounding archipelagos. Like all geographic divisions of Elysium, Insulinde is bounded by the pale, a rarefied expanse of non-matter and sensory suspension that separates it from Mundi, Seol, Graad, Samara, Katla, and the southern isolas.

Geographically, the isola is dominated by the massive Sea of Insulinde, a body of cold water subjected to persistent westerly weather systems and maritime squalls. Its primary political, cultural, and demographic center is the metropolis of Revachol, constructed along the northern coast of Le Caillou flanking the wide natural harbor of the Bay of Revachol. The climate of Insulinde is predominantly oceanic and temperate, featuring overcast skies, frequent coastal drizzle, damp winter slush, and short, humid summers.

Following its initial colonization under the royal banner of the Suzerainty of Revachol, the isola grew into the premier industrial and financial pivot of global maritime commerce. Millions of settlers, workers, and administrators migrated across the pale from Mundi and Graad, establishing vast rail yards, commercial docks, and naval fortifications. This prosperity was shattered during the Antecentennial War and the subsequent Revolution of '02, which culminated in the Coalition naval bombardment of '08. In the modern era, the Insulindian Isola exists under foreign Coalition military administration, its waters patrolled by foreign warships and its economy dependent on international shipping lines.
