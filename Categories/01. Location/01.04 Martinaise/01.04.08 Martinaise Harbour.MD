Title:
Martinaise Harbour

Text:
The Martinaise Harbour is a massive, heavily industrialized commercial deep-water shipping terminal that occupies the northern and eastern quadrants of the district, operated as a key logistics hub for the export and import of raw goods across Insulinde. Engineered to handle large ocean-going freighters, the facility encompasses hundreds of acres of poured-concrete container yards, elevated crane tracks, rail spurs, and massive gantry cranes that dominate the coastal skyline. Access to the harbor is strictly controlled through a fortified main gate equipped with hydraulic turnstiles, barbed wire fencing, and a reinforced security booth. Under normal operations, the harbor processes thousands of shipping containers daily, transferring freight from maritime cargo holds directly onto the regional railway network. However, the facility has been brought to a complete standstill by an indefinite general strike initiated by the Debardeurs Union, led by the influential Claire brothers. Rows of mammoth cargo cranes stand frozen above idle freighters, and miles of articulated container trucks remain parked along the access roads, turning the harbor into an economic fortress and a flashpoint of political confrontation between organized labor and international capital.
