Title:
Church of the Dolorian

Text:
The Church of the Dolorian is an imposing, solitary religious monument situated on the rocky western coast of Martinaise, surrounded by salt marshes and wind-twisted pine trees. Erected centuries ago during the height of Dolorian spiritual devotion, the structure is constructed from heavy cut granite blocks in a severe, monastic architectural style, featuring towering pointed arches, flying buttresses, and a cavernous central nave. A massive circular stained-glass window dominates the eastern facade, depicting stylized rays of divine light representing the innocence of Dolores Dei. Inside the cavernous sanctuary, an uncanny acoustic phenomenon occurs: sounds produced within the nave echo and dissipate in an unnatural fashion, creating an eerie silence at the exact center of the building.
