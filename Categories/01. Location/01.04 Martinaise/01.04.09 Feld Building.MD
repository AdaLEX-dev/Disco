Title:
Feld Building

Text:
The Feld Building is an enormous, abandoned industrial manufacturing and administrative complex located along the northwestern coastal bluff of Martinaise, overlooking the bay. Originally constructed by the Feld Electrical Company during Revachol industrial golden age, the building is a masterwork of early modernist industrial architecture, featuring a massive reinforced concrete frame, brick cladding, and towering rows of multi-paned factory windows. The company once manufactured cutting-edge magnetic tape systems, radiocomputers, and domestic electrical appliances that were exported throughout the isolas. Following economic decline and the outbreak of the revolution, the complex was evacuated and heavily fortified, serving as a frontline stronghold where intense urban combat took place. After the war, the Feld Electrical Company went bankrupt, leaving the complex to deteriorate into an empty shell. Its sprawling interior comprises vast assembly halls littered with shattered glass, rusted machinery mounts, flooded basement vaults, and a maze of overhead catwalks. The exterior walls are defaced by decades of political graffiti, bullet marks, and ivy growth, while sea winds howl through the hollow window frames, making it an imposing landmark of the district industrial past.
