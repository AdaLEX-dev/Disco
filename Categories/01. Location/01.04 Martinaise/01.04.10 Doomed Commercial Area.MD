Title:
Doomed Commercial Area

Text:
The Doomed Commercial Area is an enclosed multi-story commercial and retail galleria situated immediately adjacent to the Whirling-in-Rags in central Martinaise. Housed within a cavernous antecentennial masonry structure that once accommodated luxury department stores and professional offices, the complex has acquired a legendary reputation among local residents for bringing inevitable financial ruin and structural misfortune to any business that attempts to operate within its walls. Across several decades, a succession of enterprises including bakeries, gymnasiums, equestrian clothing shops, novelty dice makers, and a computer software collective have established operations in the building, only to collapse into sudden bankruptcy, madness, or mysterious physical accidents. The interior features a maze of dimly lit corridors, locked iron security grates, unfinished drywall partitions, and boarded-up shop fronts. A pervasive chill lingers in the unheated air, accompanied by creaking floorboards and strange drafts circulating through subterranean storage cellars. Despite the building notoriety, a handful of eccentric tenants continue to occupy isolated workshops in its upper levels, operating in quiet defiance of the perceived curse that hangs over the complex.
