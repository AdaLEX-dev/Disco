Title:
RCM Headquarters Precinct 41

Text:
Precinct 41 of the Revachol Citizens Militia serves as the primary law enforcement headquarters for the vast and turbulent district of Jamrock in West Revachol. Housed in a fortified antecentennial municipal building that has survived multiple wars and urban skirmishes, the precinct station reflects the precarious, underfunded, and overburdened reality of the militia. The interior is characterized by peeling paint, dim fluorescent lighting, banks of mechanical filing cabinets stuffed with case dockets, and the continuous clatter of typewriters and telegraph printers. Formed after the collapse of formal police institutions following the defeat of the Commune, the RCM operates as a volunteer peacekeeping corps recognized by the Coalition but denied official state status or modern military weaponry. Precinct 41 is staffed by a weary cohort of patrolmen, forensic investigators, and detective lieutenants who navigate severe psychological burnout, alcoholism, and high occupational mortality while attempting to maintain basic civil order across volatile neighborhoods where organized crime and armed political factions operate openly.
