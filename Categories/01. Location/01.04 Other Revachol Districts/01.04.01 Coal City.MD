Title:
Coal City

Text:
Coal City is an expansive, heavy industrial manufacturing district situated in eastern Revachol on the island of Le Caillou within the Insulindian Isola. Positioned adjacent to major railway switching yards and the eastern commercial waterways, Coal City was engineered during the height of the industrial revolution to serve as the metallurgical, energy, and heavy fabrication engine supporting the expansion of the Revacholian merchant marine and colonial infrastructure.

The physical environment of Coal City is characterized by towering blast furnaces, coking batteries, sprawling iron foundries, and mountainous stockpiles of raw bituminous coal transported across the pale from mines in Katla and Graad. A perpetual blanket of dense, sulfurous soot and industrial smoke hovers over the district, staining brick rowhouses and concrete factory walls in shades of greasy black. The streets are paved with crushed slag and cobblestones, crisscrossed by industrial rail spurs and overhead steam pipes that hiss continuously into the freezing coastal air. In the shadow of the foundries, barren slag heaps rise like artificial hills, where children forage for unburned anthracite to fuel domestic hearths.

The population consists almost entirely of metallurgical workers, furnace stokers, heavy machinery mechanics, and their families living in utilitarian company rowhouses. Shift whistles echo across the valley at dawn and dusk, signaling the turnover of thousands of laborers marching in heavy wooden-soled boots. Chronic exposure to airborne particulate has produced severe respiratory afflictions among residents. Despite the collapse of heavy export markets following the Coalition intervention, many foundries continue to operate under private enterprise syndicates, supplying structural steel to the reconstruction projects of West Revachol.
