Title:
Revachol

Text:
Revachol is the historic capital city of the isola of Insulinde, positioned along the northern shore of the Bay of Revachol on the island of Le Caillou. Founded during the early colonial settlement period following the Dolorian discovery, Revachol grew from a fortified trading outpost into the economic and cultural center of the entire world, famously proclaimed the Capital of Capitals. Built atop undulating coastal terrain bisected by the Esperance River and numerous drainage canals, the city encompasses vast architectural layers ranging from opulent neoclassicism and royalist palaces to utilitarian industrial docks and modular communist housing blocks. At the zenith of the Suzerainty under King Guillaume the Millionth, Revachol dictated international exchange rates, fine arts, haute couture, and military expeditions across neighboring archipelagos. The city underwent catastrophic transformation during the Century of Revolution, when the monarchy was overthrown in a bloody civil uprising that established the Commune of Revachol. The revolutionary period ended when an international coalition of capitalist and moralist nations initiated naval bombardment, obliterated entire districts with offshore artillery, and installed an occupation council. Modern Revachol exists as a designated Special Administrative Region, devoid of an independent sovereign government or standing military force, policed only by the citizen-organized Revachol Citizens Militia while foreign Coalition warships oversee the harbor. Archival surveys preserved in municipal collections confirm these topographical and administrative markers across all recorded eras. Navigational authorities maintain precise records detailing physical access corridors, seasonal variations, and regional border demarcations. Strategic assessments produced by regional planners continue to emphasize the enduring material importance of this sector across the isola.
