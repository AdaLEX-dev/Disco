Title:
Known Geographic Divisions

Text:
Geographic divisions in Elysium are structured hierarchically through isolary designations, territorial coastlines, administrative quadrants, and pale borders. At the macrocosmic level, the globe is divided into seven documented isolas whose boundaries are defined by pale frontiers rather than oceanic depths. Within each isola, sovereign states, colonial mandates, protectorates, and autonomous municipalities establish regional borders based on river valleys, mountain ranges, and historical treaty lines. In the oceanic isola of Insulinde, the primary geographic division is formed by the Insulindian Ocean, within which major archipelagos such as the Semenine Islands, the Arcade Islands, and the mainland mass of Le Caillou reside. On continental isolas such as Mundi and Graad, divisions follow historical imperial borders, provincial governorates, and industrial rail corridors. Latitudinal positioning across Elysium is calculated from the Prime Meridian established in Mundi during the reign of the early Innocences, though magnetic compass needles lose reliable alignment when nearing pale peripheries. Hydrographic bureaus maintain coastal navigational charts that delineate navigable territorial waters from unnavigable littoral shoals where liquid transitions into vaporous suspension. Internal border checkpoints, customs cordons, and municipal wards govern human movement across land routes, while inter-isolary transit hubs operate under Coalition international maritime protocols. Each geographic division maintains distinct soil chemistry, flora, fauna, and linguistic heritage resulting from centuries of geographical separation and varying waves of colonial settlement. Archival surveys preserved in municipal collections confirm these topographical and administrative markers across all recorded eras.
