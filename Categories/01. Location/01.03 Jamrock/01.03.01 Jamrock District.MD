Title:
Jamrock District

Text:
Jamrock is a massive, densely populated metropolitan district situated in western Revachol on the island of Le Caillou within the Insulindian Isola. Sprawling across coastal lowlands and urban valleys south of the Bay of Revachol, Jamrock forms the populous, beating heart of the Revacholian working class. Originally developed during the industrial expansion of the nineteenth century, the district was engineered to accommodate hundreds of thousands of factory laborers, rail workers, and immigrant families who arrived from Mundi, Graad, and Seol to labor in the capital's booming manufacturing sectors.

The architectural landscape of Jamrock is defined by dense rows of multi-story red brick tenements, weathered concrete apartment blocks, narrow cobblestone alleys, and elevated railway viaducts. Heavy soot from factory smokestacks and coal-burning home stoves coats the masonry in a uniform dark patina, mingling with the perpetual coastal drizzle that sweeps in from the bay. Beneath the paved streets lies an extensive, decaying subterranean network of century-old brick sewer tunnels and stormwater canals, many of which collapsed during the Coalition bombardment and now overflow into basement foundations during winter storms.

In the contemporary post-revolutionary era, Jamrock suffers from chronic poverty, severe municipal underfunding, and high rates of violent crime. Law enforcement across the district is shared uneasily between Precinct 41 and Precinct 57 of the Revachol Citizens Militia, whose under-resourced patrol officers navigate labyrinthine alleys contested by street gangs, narcotics trafficking networks controlled by the shadowy La Puta Madre syndicate, and neighborhood defense councils. Despite its harsh material deprivation and social fractures, Jamrock sustains a resilient cultural life centered on corner cafes, unlicensed radio stations, and lively street markets.
