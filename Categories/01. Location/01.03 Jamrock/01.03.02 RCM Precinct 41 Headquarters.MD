Title:
RCM Precinct 41 Headquarters

Text:
RCM Precinct 41 Headquarters is the operational base of the 41st precinct of the Revachol Citizens Militia, situated in an imposing, repurposed brick industrial building in the Jamrock quarter of West Revachol. Known informally throughout the militia as the Forty-First or the Jamrock Station, the precinct oversees law enforcement across several difficult, densely populated urban sectors including Jamrock, Capeside, and outlying industrial fringes bordering the western bay.

The station house occupies a former nineteenth-century silk mill and municipal warehouse that was hastily requisitioned by neighborhood militias during the post-bombardment reconstruction. The interior is characterized by worn oak floorboards, stained plaster walls, and a pervasive smell of stale chicory coffee, tobacco smoke, wet wool coats, and carbon paper. The main floor contains open bullpen desks cluttered with mechanical typewriters, rotary telephones, rolled precinct blueprints, and official incident ledgers, where patrolmen and lieutenants draft arrest reports beneath buzzing filament lamps. A rooftop lattice radio mast coordinates motorized patrols navigating the rain-slick streets.

Under the leadership of Captain Pryce, Precinct 41 has developed a reputation for gritty pragmatism, institutional cynicism, and tactical competence in handling high-risk urban homicide investigations. The station's Major Crimes Unit, which includes veteran detectives such as Jean Vicquemare, Judit Minot, Trant Heidelstam, and Chester McLaine, handles complex investigations across contested gang territories with minimal support from Coalition authorities. The basement vaults house reinforced holding cells, forensic evidence lockers, and a motor carriage garage where battered Coupris Kineema patrol vehicles are serviced by militia mechanics amid pooling motor oil and rainwater.
