Title:
Terminal

Text:
The Terminal district is an expansive logistical and transit hub located in southern Revachol, centered around the massive convergence of maritime docks, freight rail marshaling yards, and inter-district truck terminals. Engineered to facilitate the rapid movement of goods, fuel, and industrial machinery across the isola, Terminal is defined by endless rows of poured-concrete storage warehouses, petroleum storage tanks, and multi-track railway switches that operate day and night. The district atmosphere is dominated by the roar of heavy diesel locomotives, the rhythmic clanging of shunting cargo cars, and the hiss of pneumatic air brakes. Few permanent residential neighborhoods exist within Terminal; the population consists primarily of shift workers, locomotive engineers, freight handlers, and logistics clerks who work for transport syndicates or municipal transit authorities. Roads are broad, straight, and heavily scarred by continuous heavy axle traffic, while perimeter fences topped with barbed wire secure commercial freight compounds against unauthorized access and cargo theft. Archival surveys preserved in municipal collections confirm these topographical and administrative markers across all recorded eras. Navigational authorities maintain precise records detailing physical access corridors, seasonal variations, and regional border demarcations. Strategic assessments produced by regional planners continue to emphasize the enduring material importance of this sector across the isola.
