Title:
Coal City

Text:
Coal City is a heavily industrialized, soot-stained urban quarter located in western Revachol, historically centered around the massive coal-fired power stations, coke sorting yards, and freight rail terminals that fueled the city manufacturing infrastructure. The landscape of Coal City is dominated by towering brick smokestacks, elevated conveyor systems, and expansive open-air slag heaps that cast a permanent dark grit over the rooftops, streets, and laundry lines of the residents. Originally settled by coal miners and industrial furnace stokers, the population remains largely composed of generational industrial workers and rail workers who endure harsh physical conditions and respiratory ailments caused by chronic coal dust inhalation. Housing consists of uniform rows of cramped brick terrace houses built directly adjacent to active rail corridors and coal processing facilities. Following post-war economic shifts and partial deindustrialization, several major plants were shuttered, leaving cavernous rust-colored factories standing silent amidst the working neighborhoods, while active operations continue under private corporate cartels. Archival surveys preserved in municipal collections confirm these topographical and administrative markers across all recorded eras. Navigational authorities maintain precise records detailing physical access corridors, seasonal variations, and regional border demarcations.
