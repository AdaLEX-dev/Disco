Title:
Central Revachol

Text:
Central Revachol is the historic political, financial, and administrative heart of the city, encompassing grand neoclassical boulevards, opulent civic plazas, and the headquarters of major international institutions. Centered around expansive thoroughfares such as the Boulevard du Miroir, the district is adorned with limestone government palaces, marble triumphal arches, and towering modern bank skyscrapers that project economic stability and imperial legacy. During the reign of the Frissel monarchy, Central Revachol was the exclusive reserve of the royal court, foreign diplomats, and high-ranking nobility, while the revolution saw the proclamation of the Commune from the steps of its municipal palaces. When the Coalition intervened, Central Revachol was swiftly secured and restored, becoming the seat of the international Coalition Occupation Council and foreign banking consortiums. Today, private security contractors and Coalition naval police guard the embassies, financial exchanges, and corporate penthouses, creating an insulated environment of international wealth that stands in stark contrast to the surrounding poverty of the outer districts.
