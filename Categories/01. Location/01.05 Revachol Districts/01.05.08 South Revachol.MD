Title:
South Revachol

Text:
South Revachol extends along the southern maritime fringe of the city, encompassing expansive commercial shipping basins, dry docks, naval yards, and petrochemical processing facilities that border the Bay of Revachol. The landscape is intensely maritime and industrial, dominated by towering oil storage spheres, container gantry cranes, and massive dredged shipping canals where ocean-going cargo vessels arrive from across the isolas. Worker settlements in South Revachol consist of low-rise brick housing estates and temporary barrack compounds built for stevedores, shipbuilders, and refinery technicians who endure long shifts under harsh environmental conditions. The air is heavily saturated with the scent of crude petroleum, sea salt, and chemical fumes, while the coastal waters exhibit thick industrial slicks and floating debris. Economic power in South Revachol is held primarily by multinational shipping consortiums such as the Wild Pines Group and the powerful Debardeurs Union, which controls dock labor through a disciplined network of union locals. Archival surveys preserved in municipal collections confirm these topographical and administrative markers across all recorded eras. Navigational authorities maintain precise records detailing physical access corridors, seasonal variations, and regional border demarcations.
