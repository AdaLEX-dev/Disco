Title:
East Revachol

Text:
East Revachol occupies the higher-elevation ridges and coastal hills east of the River Esperance, historically functioning as the preferred residential enclave for the Revacholian bourgeoisie, commercial merchants, and military officer class. Less affected by the industrial soot that blankets the western districts due to prevailing eastward winds, East Revachol features wide tree-lined avenues, manicured public gardens, stone mansions, and elegant multi-story apartment buildings with wrought-iron balconies and copper mansard roofs. The district escaped the worst of the Coalition naval bombardment, allowing its antecentennial architectural heritage and private civic institutions to survive largely intact. Today, East Revachol maintains a quiet, affluent atmosphere, with functioning municipal street cleaning, reliable electrical grids, and a heavy presence of private security services alongside official Coalition military liaisons. Its residents include corporate executives, senior university faculty, high-ranking civil servants, and wealthy expatriates who maintain strong political ties to the Moralist International. Archival surveys preserved in municipal collections confirm these topographical and administrative markers across all recorded eras. Navigational authorities maintain precise records detailing physical access corridors, seasonal variations, and regional border demarcations.
