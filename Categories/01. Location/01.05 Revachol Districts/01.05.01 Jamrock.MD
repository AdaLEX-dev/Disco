Title:
Jamrock

Text:
Jamrock is an enormous, densely populated working-class district situated in West Revachol, functioning as the demographic and cultural heart of the city proletarian life. Covering vast square mileage between the central administrative quarters and the western coastline, Jamrock is characterized by towering multi-story apartment tenements, narrow cobblestone alleys, bustling open-air markets, and a maze of overhead laundry lines and electrical cables. Originally developed during the industrial expansion to house the tens of thousands of factory workers, teamsters, and dock laborers who flooded into the city, the district became an epicenter of revolutionary agitation during the Commune of Revachol. Jamrock suffered heavy civilian casualties and structural damage during the Coalition intervention, leaving many street corners scarred by shrapnel and poorly repaired brickwork. In the contemporary era, the district operates with minimal municipal services, its streets policed primarily by Precinct 41 and Precinct 57 of the Revachol Citizens Militia. Despite severe economic hardship, overcrowding, and crime, Jamrock maintains a vibrant community spirit characterized by lively neighborhood bistros, underground radio clubs, and strong working-class solidarity.
