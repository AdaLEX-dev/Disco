Title:
The Isola of Graad

Text:
Graad is an immense continental isola situated in the cold northern latitudes of Elysium, characterized by vast expanses of boreal taiga, permafrost tundra, rugged mountain ranges, and deep freshwater lakes. Historically dominated by an absolute imperial dynasty that ruled through an aristocratic feudal hierarchy and formidable military legions, Graad became the birthplace of modern communist theory and revolutionary upheaval during the early decades of the twentieth century. The Graadian Revolution violently overthrew the imperial autocracy, establishing the first workers republic and inspiring socialist uprisings across the world, including the Commune of Revachol. Following decades of internal ideological purges, civil conflicts, and economic centralization, modern Graad has transformed into a bureaucratic state dominated by party apparatuses, state security organizations, and massive industrial extraction combines that export oil, timber, and heavy machinery. The culture of Graad is severe, disciplined, and deeply intellectual, characterized by rich traditions in classical music, chess, ideological philosophy, and mechanical engineering that exert widespread international influence despite political friction with western moralist nations.
