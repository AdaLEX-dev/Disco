Title:
The Innocence Succession Doctrine in Revachol

Text:
The Innocence Succession Doctrine constitutes the supreme theological, philosophical, and historiographical framework that has structured civilizational thought across Elysium for centuries. According to this profound doctrinal belief, human history does not progress in a random or purely material fashion, but is periodically guided and elevated by the physical emergence of an Innocence, a living historical avatar endowed with infallible political foresight, transcendent charisma, and divine transformative authority over human society.

An Innocence is recognized by miraculous somatic signs, most iconically exemplified by Innocence Dolores Dei, whose lungs shone with a brilliant interior luminescence through her chest wall, and by their singular capacity to fundamentally alter the course of science, jurisprudence, and historical reality. The decree of a reigning Innocence is held to possess absolute moral and legal validity, superseding all existing constitutions, national boundaries, and sovereign crowns. Historic eras are officially dated according to the reign of each recognized Innocence, whose divine interventions established modern linguistics, naval exploration, and international moralist governance.

In modern Revachol, the doctrine faces a profound civilizational crisis. Over two centuries have elapsed since the martyrdom of Dolores Dei, and no new Innocence has appeared to assume global succession. This prolonged absence, referred to by theologians as the Great Interregnum, has engendered deep spiritual despair across the Insulindian Isola, coinciding with the violent destruction of the monarchy, the failure of the Commune, and the physical thickening of the pale. Scholars and ordinary citizens openly debate whether the succession has permanently ended, leaving humanity abandoned to face the slow dissolution of the world alone.
