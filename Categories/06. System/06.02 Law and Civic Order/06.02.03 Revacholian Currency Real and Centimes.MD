Title:
Revacholian Currency Real and Centimes

Text:
The Revacholian Real and its fractional centimes constitute the official monetary and transactional medium circulating throughout the city of Revachol and the surrounding Insulindian Isola. Divided into one hundred centimes per real, the currency is the modern economic descendant of the pre-revolutionary royalist livre and the short lived communard labor credit. Following the monetary stabilization decrees enforced by the Moralintern in the aftermath of the Coalition invasion, the real was restructured as a fiat currency pegged directly to foreign reserve baskets managed by the central banking institutions of Mundi and Graad.

Physical currency circulates in the form of mass produced paper banknotes and stamped metal coinage. Paper notes feature sober moralist motifs, historic Dolorian architectural facades, and allegorical engravings of industry and commerce, while copper, nickel, and bronze coins circulate in denominations of ten, twenty, and fifty centimes. Due to decades of chronic deindustrialization, high municipal unemployment, and predatory foreign import tariffs, cash liquidity remains severely restricted across the working class districts of Jamrock, Coal City, and Martinaise, where ordinary citizens struggle to secure basic foodstuffs, tobacco, and medicines.

In impoverished peripheral enclaves, the scarcity of physical currency has given rise to robust informal micro-economies. Empty glass bottles and tare recyclables operate as common street level currencies, exchanged at automated return machines for small quantities of centimes to purchase alcohol or pay hostel tariffs. The stark contrast between the desperate coin counting of local dockworkers and the multi-thousand real bank transfers executed by foreign shipping executives highlights the deep economic stratification enforced by the post-war monetary order of Revachol.
