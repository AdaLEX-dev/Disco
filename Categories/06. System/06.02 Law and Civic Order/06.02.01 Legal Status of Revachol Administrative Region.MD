Title:
Legal Status of Revachol Administrative Region

Text:
The Legal Status of the Revachol Administrative Region defines the complex, non-sovereign constitutional framework under which the metropolis has been governed since the violent destruction of the Revachol Commune in Year '08. Following the military conquest of the Insulindian Isola, the victorious Moralist Coalition declined to restore the historic Frissel monarchy or recognize an autonomous civilian republic. Instead, the foreign powers promulgated the Coalition Administrative Charter, designating Revachol as a special extraterritorial protectorate directly subordinate to international occupational oversight.

Under this legal framework, Revachol is deliberately denied the instruments of modern statehood. The city possesses no sovereign constitution, no elected municipal parliament, and no civilian mayor with statutory executive power. Sovereign authority is vested entirely in the Coalition Council of Regional Commissioners, a body of foreign technocrats and diplomats who administer territorial commerce, taxation, and police licensing from fortified administrative enclaves. The territory operates as a deregulated free trade zone where multinational shipping cartels and financial conglomerates enjoy total immunity from local corporate taxation and labor regulations.

This imperial structure has created an acute jurisdictional vacuum across the metropolis. While Coalition courts aggressively protect international maritime property, contract rights, and military installations, they completely abdicate responsibility for everyday civilian civil law and neighborhood protection. As a consequence, domestic order is precariously contested between the underfunded Revachol Citizens Militia, militant trade syndicates like the Debardeurs Union, and organized criminal syndicates, leaving the civilian populace trapped in a stateless limbo of institutional disenfranchisement.
